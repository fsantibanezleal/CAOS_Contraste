- G20 contract 1: the `scored_sample` family gains a declared population sample (`contract-family` 1.2.0 to 1.3.0, additive, minor). The dataset parameter `outcome` takes a third value, `population`: target and decision are both optional and a row that carries a value in either is rejected with the rule `SS-POPULATION-NO-OUTCOME` ("A population sample carries neither an outcome nor a decision; its role is a shift probe."), so a population sample is never validated as a scored one. `target` and `none` behave exactly as before. The declaration `data/derived/contract/scored_sample.json`, the catalog row and history row and `docs/contracts/versions.lock.json` carry 1.3.0; the readiness is regenerated (its catalog hash only).
- G20 C20: `cases/c20/fed.py` reads the three Federal Reserve 2025 corporate portfolios through contract 1 as a population sample (`contract_records`, `validate_portfolio`, `CONTRACT_FIELDS`, `CONTRACT_RULES`), replacing the open question of U14-P2. Named conventions: `id` is the source `loan_id` as an integer string and `observation_date` is `2025-12-31`, the end of the year of the 2025 stress test methodology data. The twelve loan variables are declared with units (US dollars; the rate as a decimal fraction, its basis UNVERIFIED; the term in months with `NA` flagged and never imputed; codes as `int`; the rating as `str` with the rule `C20-FED-RATING` on `RATING_SCALE`). The bake runs `validate_portfolio` on each portfolio before the drift blocks and stops on any rejected row, naming the file and the row. The C20 documents are byte-identical (seven `data/derived/C20/*.json` hashes compared before and after a full bake on the real files).
- G20 tests: `tests/contracts/test_population_sample.py` and three tests in `tests/c20/test_c20_readers.py`.
