- U9-P2 regulatory parity bake: data-pipeline/pipeline/regulatory/ (bake.py, inputs.json, `python -m pipeline.regulatory`) runs the official worked examples and the invariant cases through riskvalidation.regulatory and writes data/derived/regulatory/calculators.json, the regulatory-calculators document the live ports of the regulation page and of C12 are held to at 1e-9 relative. The five CRE99 netting sets are computed from their transcribed trade inputs, never copied (EAD 569.470141, 381.238319, 5,405.615982, 936.450506 and 1,879.212632, rounding to 569, 381, 5,406, 936 and 1,879; multiplier 0.965208 for set 2); the RBC20.13 floor gives 101.5 and 95 with the RBC90.2 cap; the grid holds 79 CRE20 cells, the phase-in six RBC90.1 rows and the four parameter tables the engine's own data, each with its paragraph and in-force date. The invariant cases are BA-CVA (rho = 1, the hedge floor), the SBM scenarios, aggregation and cross-bucket fallback, the liquidity-horizon cascade, and a GIRR delta case whose headline divides the risk weights by sqrt(2) for USD (MAR21.44, a named convention) with the undivided charge beside it. The bake calls check_regulatory on the document and refuses it on any finding; two bakes are byte-identical.
- U9-P2 engine pin: data-pipeline/requirements.txt pins riskvalidation to the reviewed batch 3 commit 25e9538faf10bfdbcde03c5adccffd8e86ec275c until the batch 3 release is tagged (it pinned v0.07.000). Registry rows: none, the bake reads no third-party data file, so data-pipeline/config/sources.json, licences.json, data/sources/manifest.json and the raw-source record gain no row; the provenance names the generator regulatory-parity-bake and the engine commit.
- U9-P2 findings for the engine (not blockers; the contract checker accepts the document): F1 and F2, no engine function returns the systematic and idiosyncratic terms of CRE52.61 (printed 2,253 and 77,344 in CRE99.54), so the bake derives them from the engine's supervisory factors and correlations and refuses the document unless sqrt(term_1 + term_2) equals the engine's credit_addon to 1e-12; F3, cross_bucket_charge returns the charge without a flag that the MAR21.4(5)(b) fallback applied, so the bake derives the flag from the sign of the radicand and refuses the document unless, with no fallback, the engine's charge is the square root of that radicand. The recommendation is a result type from the engine for both (a minor version of engine-regulatory-api). For the credit and commodity trades the effective notional is delta x notional x supervisory_duration x maturity_factor, multiplied in the bake from the engine's two functions, as ir_addon does internally for interest-rate trades.
- U9-P2 integrator wiring: data-pipeline/run.py is the integrator's; the entry point is `pipeline.regulatory.bake.bake(output_root)` (it writes `<output_root>/regulatory/calculators.json` and returns the manifest entry) and `python -m pipeline.regulatory [--output-root DIR]` from data-pipeline/. The document is written with its numbers exact (never rounded), because the ports are held to it at 1e-9 relative; its code_version is the constant regulatory-parity-bake/1, so a VERSION bump does not stale the committed bytes.
