# Case `C03`, SME PD by vintage through 2008, on public small-business loans

- **Category:** `credit-scoring`
- **Source:** real sample: the SBA 7(a) FOIA loan files (U.S. Small Business Administration, as of 2026-06-30, U.S.
  Government Works); derived documents only.
- **One line:** five PD models, each trained only on the approval years whose 5-year outcome was fully observed before its
  test cohort started, tested on that cohort at each vintage's 5-year mark.

## Why this case exists

It is the question a model risk function asks of any PD model built on past cohorts: does it stay calibrated for the cohorts that
follow, and does it say so honestly when the cohort lived through a crisis? A model built on the vintages FY1991-2000 is
tested on FY2006-07; one built through FY2002 on FY2008-10; and so on to FY2018-20. A PD model has two jobs, ranking borrowers and
getting the level right, and the case shows that they come apart: the ranking holds across the crisis, the level does not.

The loans are **SBA 7(a) small-business loans made by lenders under a federal guarantee, not a bank's SME book**. The file has
no delinquency history, no balance and no rating, records the interest rate only from the FY2008 approvals, and says nothing of
the borrowers beyond the loan's amount, guaranteed share, term, sector and whether it is revolving. A charge-off is the default.
What a validator learns from the case is how the tests behave on a real vintage panel with a crisis in the middle; the PD
levels are those of this guarantee program and are not a statement about any bank.

## Formalization

| Parameter | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Vintage | approval fiscal year, 1 October to 30 September | the cohort a loan belongs to |
| Target | a charge-off within 60 months of approval | the 5-year charge-off target |
| 5-year mark | 30 September of the fiscal year plus 5 | the date from which a vintage's target is read; before it the observed value is null with its reason |
| Test cohorts (variants) | FY2006-07, FY2008-10, FY2011-14, FY2015-17, FY2018-20 | the approval years a model is tested on |
| Training window (decision D1) | FY1991 to FY(first fiscal year of the cohort - 6), outcomes read up to 30 September of the year before the cohort | strict out of time: no outcome of a training loan is seen early |
| Inputs | log amount, guaranteed share, term, sector group, revolving | origination attributes only; the rate is not an input |
| Rungs | constant, WoE scorecard (champion), L1 logit on WoE, PLTR, EBM, monotone LightGBM | the existing rungs of the C01 ladder, fitted on a stratified sample of 40,000 loans of each window |
| TabPFN | absent | the optional extra is not installed in the bake environment; no number stands in for it |
| Validation | `riskvalidation` 0.5.0 under its default policy | AUC against the calibration slice, DeLong, Jeffreys, binomial, Vasicek-corrected binomial (asset correlation 0.12), Hosmer-Lemeshow, Spiegelhalter, PSI |
| Curve shape | the share of the window's 5-year charge-offs falling by month 12, 24, 36, 48, 60 | the model's 5-year PD allocated in time for the predicted vintage curves |

## Training window of each variant

Each variant trains only on the approval years whose 5-year charge-off mark falls before the first approval date of its test cohort.

| Variant | Training window | Outcomes read up to | Trained through the crisis |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2006-07 | FY1991 to FY2000 | 2005-09-30 | no |
| FY2008-10 | FY1991 to FY2002 | 2007-09-30 | no: the window closes before the recession |
| FY2011-14 | FY1991 to FY2005 | 2010-09-30 | yes: it holds the charge-offs dated in the recession |
| FY2015-17 | FY1991 to FY2009 | 2014-09-30 | yes |
| FY2018-20 | FY1991 to FY2012 | 2017-09-30 | yes |

The flag is computed from the documents: a window is "trained through the crisis" when it holds a 5-year charge-off dated between
December 2007 and June 2009 (the NBER recession).

What the validator finds, read from the table and the documents:

- **FY2006-07 (trained to FY2000):** the 5-year charge-off rate of the training window is 7.1%; the cohort's is 25.0% against a predicted
  8.8%, a gap of 16.2 points. The one-sided level tests (Jeffreys, binomial) are red; the scorecard still ranks well
  (AUC 0.825), so the failure is the level, not the ranking. The stability test of the PD distribution is red.
- **FY2008-10 (trained to FY2002, before the recession):** FY2008 observed 21.3% against 7.4% predicted, FY2009 8.4% against 6.2%,
  FY2010 4.9% against 5.4%: the model understates the early vintages and is about right for the last, so the cohort total
  (13.1% against 6.5%) hides the swing. The level tests are red, the AUC stays at 0.815. The window holds no crisis outcome.
- **FY2011-14 (trained through the crisis):** the training rate is 8.1% and the cohort's observed rate 3.4% against 6.4%
  predicted: the model overstates by 3.0 points. The one-sided tests that look for an understated PD (Jeffreys, binomial) are
  green, as they should be; the two-sided Hosmer-Lemeshow is red.
- **FY2015-17 and FY2018-20:** the training windows (12.3% and 11.2%) carry the crisis vintages; the cohorts' observed rates are
  4.0% and 3.9% against 8.4% and 6.1% predicted, an overstatement of 4.4 and 2.2 points. The AUC of the FY2015-17 scorecard is below
  its AUC on the calibration slice (red); the FY2018-20 AUC is 0.826.
- **Rungs:** no rung is uniformly best. The best AUC is the L1 logit in FY2006-07 and FY2018-20 (0.826 and 0.834, within a point
  of the scorecard), the monotone gradient boosting model in FY2008-10 and FY2011-14 (0.832, 0.873) and PLTR in FY2015-17 (0.849).
  The EBM and PLTR rungs fall below the scorecard in FY2006-07 (0.734 and 0.770 against 0.825) and in FY2018-20 (0.746 and 0.739
  against 0.826). The constant PD ranks nobody (0.5) and shows the level error on its own.

## What the data cannot say

- **Small-business loans under a federal guarantee, not a bank's SME book.** The vintages are those of the SBA 7(a) program; the
  PD level of the program is not a bank's. Two loans with the same inputs are the same to every rung, and nothing of the borrower's
  financial statements or behaviour is in the file.
- **Right-censoring.** The target of a loan is read at an observation date. A loan observed for the whole 60 months without a
  charge-off is a non-event, whether it was paid, charged off later or is still active (the file shows a charge-off as CHGOFF, never
  as an active loan). A loan observed for less than 60 months and not charged off has no outcome: it is right-censored, counted apart
  (`n_exempt_censored`) and never counted as performing. At the files' as-of date (2026-06-30) the 5-year mark of every vintage of
  the five cohorts has passed (the last, FY2020, on 2025-09-30), so no vintage of a cohort is censored; the censoring rule governs
  the training windows (all closed before their cutoff) and the vintages after FY2020, which no cohort tests. Loans still active after
  60 months are non-events: they are under 1% of the FY2006-07 cohort and about 29% of the FY2018-20 cohort, the share the documents carry as
  `active_nonevent_share`.
- **The curve shape is the training window's.** The predicted cumulative PD at 12 to 48 months is the champion's 5-year PD allocated
  by the window's timing of charge-offs; only the 5-year value is a model output.
- **A sample of the window.** The rungs are fitted on a stratified sample of 40,000 loans of each window (the windows hold 330,682 to
  1,024,597 loans); the window's counts, the sample size and the seed are in every model record.
- **The Vasicek-corrected binomial** uses an asset correlation of 0.12, the one-factor value of dossier 02 B.4; it is an assumption
  of the test, not a fitted value, and the other level tests do not depend on it.

## Validation anchor

The PD tests are the engine's: the product defines none (`tests/c03/test_engine_calls.py`). A model never sees anything after the
cutoff of its window: the test perturbs every approval and every outcome after the cutoff and the labels and every rung are
bit-identical (`tests/c03/test_cutoff.py`). The 5-year value of a vintage exists only at its mark (`tests/c03/test_target.py`).
The documents validate against `c03-outputs` and `c03-models`, the manifest lists each with its exact size, and a re-bake with the
same seed reproduces every document (`tests/c03/test_documents.py`).

## Notes

- Small-business loans under a federal guarantee, not a bank's SME book; the PD levels are the program's.
- The interest rate is recorded only from the FY2008 approvals (51% of the loans have none) and is not a model input.
- No delinquency, balance or rating in the file; a default is a charge-off, and a charge-off after the horizon is not an event.
- The TabPFN challenger is declared absent; its optional extra is not installed in the bake environment.
- The expected ranges of the bake were declared from the charge-off counts per fiscal year before the rungs existed.
