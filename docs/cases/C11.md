# Case `C11`, market VaR and ES back-testing across 2008, 2020, 2022 and 2023

- **Category:** `market-ccr` (market and counterparty risk)
- **Source:** real public daily series: the Kenneth French daily factors (derived-only), the US Treasury daily par yield curve
  and the Federal Reserve H.10 euro rates; derived documents only.
- **One line:** a ladder of seventeen VaR and ES models forecasts one day ahead through six windows, strictly rolling, and
  a validator asks which model is eligible, which beats the champion under a consistent score and whether exceedances cluster.

## Why this case exists

It is the market risk case on real outcomes. A VaR model that passes in a calm year can fail in a crisis, and a test that
looks only at the count of exceedances misses their timing and the size of the loss beyond the VaR. C11 forecasts the 99 and
97.5 percent VaR and the 97.5 percent ES of seventeen models on public series, scores them with the consistent FZ0 loss, and
sets traditional back-tests as eligibility gates before the comparison. The calm window 2004 to 2006 is the control: the
same mixed book as the 2023 window, so the crisis behaviour of every class can be read against one calm baseline.

## Formalization

| Parameter | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Windows (forecast days, inclusive) | calm 2004-01-02 to 2006-12-29; equity 2007-07-02 to 2009-06-30; FX 2008-07-01 to 2009-06-30; equity 2020-02-03 to 2020-06-30; rates 2022-01-03 to 2022-12-30; mixed 2023-03-01 to 2023-05-31 | the dates of decision D2; a window has no lead-in |
| Portfolios | equity: the US market; rates: a long 10-year Treasury par bond (return = minus modified duration times the yield change); FX: long euro against the dollar; mixed (2023 and calm): 50 percent equity, 30 percent bond, 20 percent euro, rebalanced daily | decision D4 gives the calm window the mixed book |
| Scheme | one-step forecast of day t from the returns before t only | the estimation window of every day ends before the day |
| Hyperparameters | fixed once on 1995-01-01 to 2003-12-31 | window length, EWMA and age-weight decay, EVT threshold fraction; never tuned on a test window |
| Levels | 99 percent (the Basel level) and 97.5 percent (the FRTB ES level) | tail probabilities 0.01 and 0.025 |
| Refit | GARCH family, filtered HS, EVT and the CAViaR ES step every day; CAViaR VaR every 5 days; GAS-FZ every 20 days | between refits the filter runs over the current window |
| Eligibility gates | Kupiec, conditional coverage and Acerbi-Szekely Z2, red at either level | the champion is the lowest average FZ0 among the eligible with an ES every day |
| Comparison | Diebold-Mariano against the champion (zero lags); 90 percent model confidence set on FZ0 | one-step forecasts |
| Engine | `riskvalidation` 0.7.0 (tag v0.07.000) with its `market` extra | every forecast, test and score |

The data are the daily US equity market return of the Kenneth French library (derived-only), the 10-year par yield of the US Treasury and the
US dollars per euro of the H.10 release. The Kenneth French library is derived-only (dossier 08 C.5): its raw file stays in the
device data root, and the documents hold forecasts, scores and test results, never the returns; the realised loss of a window
that holds it is withheld. A day missing from a series (a holiday, an empty cell) is skipped and counted, never filled.

## Expected results

The rates window should show the cost of a hiking cycle that a volatility-based model adapts to slowly; the equity windows should
punish historical simulation and the normal tail at 99 percent, while models with a heavy tail (Student-t, EVT) keep their
coverage; the calm window should leave most models eligible. The tables below are generated from the committed documents by
`python data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c11/page.py`; the test compares them with the documents.

## Validation anchor

The engine reproduces its golden values through the pin (`tests/c11/test_c11_engine_calls.py`), and a simulator with a known law
separates the true VaR from a misspecified one. Every document passes the producer checks of `tests/contracts_u8`, which
recompute the exceedances, scores and traffic lights from the stored series.

## Limits

- **The Z2 null is tail-consistent, not model-specific.** A historical, CAViaR or GAS model has no predictive distribution, so the
  simulated null draws an exceedance with probability alpha and an exponential excess whose mean is ES minus VaR. It tests the
  ES level; a heavier true tail makes the critical values too narrow.
- **The euro starts in 2000.** The H.10 euro series cannot carry a window inside the hyperparameter span, so the FX leg is not
  part of the selection, and the calm window's first forecasts use 996 days of history instead of 1,000.
- **The rates window has 249 forecast days** because the bond market is closed on more days than the stock market, so its Basel
  traffic light is not evaluated (decision D3, fewer than 250 days); the short windows (2020, 2023) are the same.
- **The bond return is first order.** Carry and convexity are left out.
- **The Du-Escanciano and McNeil-Frey tests** are in the engine but need a probability integral transform or a volatility path that
  not every rung has; C11 does not run them. The R10 network is a challenger that is not trained.
- **The ES-CAViaR is the engine's two-step estimator**, a declared deviation from Taylor's joint estimation.
- **Models are compared on their own refit schedule**; a failed fit keeps the previous one and is counted in the models document.
