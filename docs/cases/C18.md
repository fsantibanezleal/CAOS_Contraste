# Case C18, ICAAP and IAPE capital aggregation under the Fed, EBA and NGFS scenarios

- **Category:** `capital-stress`
- **Source:** real scenario paths and charge-off rates with a synthetic bank (`synthetic-calibrated`): the Federal Reserve 2026 baseline and severely adverse paths and net charge-off
  rates, the EBA/ESRB 2025 adverse scenario (United States rows), the NGFS Phase 5 Delayed transition (derived-only), the BCCh EUR to CLP rate, and the committed documents of C07, C11, C15 and C17 for Banco Andino
  (ratios only from the Banco Santander-Chile Form 20-F 2025, derived-only). Methods: `riskvalidation.aggregation`, `riskvalidation.stress` and their validation tests (engine contracts
  `engine-aggregation-api` 1.1.0 and `engine-stress-api`, pinned by the commit in `data-pipeline/requirements.txt`). Code: `data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c18/`; documents: `data/derived/C18/`;
  contracts `c18-outputs`, `c18-outputs-aggregation`, `c18-outputs-reverse` and `c18-models` 1.0.0.
- **One line:** whether Banco Andino holds its internal capital target for three years under each scenario once credit, market, IRRBB and operational risk are aggregated, and how much of the answer rests on the aggregation method.

## Executive brief

**Initiative.** The head of capital planning who owns the IAPE, the Chilean ICAAP report, must show the CMF every 30 April how the bank holds its internal capital target (OI) over three years under stress,
with the Annex 3 summary table. The IAPE adds up the capital needed for each risk by simple addition, and the ECB does not recognise inter-risk diversification in the SREP, so a validator has to say how much of
any diversified figure is the method. The reason to act now: Circular 2.365 of 2025-07-08 changes the definition of the OI and lets the CMF suggest the voluntary stress buffer (component d), from the IAPE delivered in April 2027,
and the Federal Reserve (2026-02-04), the EBA/ESRB (2025-02-26) and NGFS (Phase V, 2024-11) scenarios the bank will be asked about are already published.

**Outcomes.**

**Positioning.** The case does not say what a bank's own ICAAP returned. It reproduces the IAPE arithmetic on a synthetic bank with the engine of Contraste and shows what the aggregation method, the satellites and the scenario choice each move.

**Credibility.** Every satellite, marginal, band row, capital row, Annex 3 row and test is computed by `riskvalidation`, and each document passes its schema and the cross-field checks before it is written. The unknowns are named: the correlations of IRRBB,
the AT1/T2 split, c_P2, c_d, the PPNR ratios and the market scaling are conventions or case assumptions, listed with their basis in `models-banco-andino.json`.

## Why this case exists

An IAPE answers one question with several models: what capital each risk needs, how they add up, and what three years of stress do to it. The case separates the three choices a validator must be able to challenge: the aggregation method
(the band), the satellite that turns a macro path into a loss (the back-cast), and the scenario (four published paths). It is also the first case where the headline is deliberately not the most favourable figure.

## Formalization

The method is in [`docs/design/features/c18/design.md`](../design/features/c18/design.md): the four marginals at one year and 99.9 percent (credit by the engine's grouped copula on the C07 segments, market from C11 scaled by two conventions, IRRBB from the Hull-White simulation of C15, operational from the
Fourier aggregation of the C17 `xi-0-8` generator scaled by assets), economic capital as VaR less expected loss, the satellites (AR(1) with GDP growth and unemployment at lags 0 and 1, probit link, identity link for the two real estate series) fitted on 1985 to 2019 and run
from 2019 Q4 for the out-of-time back-cast of 2020 to 2025, the scenario paths transposed onto the last observed value (a US-calibrated adaptation), the loss-only CET1 path with a labelled PPNR row, the OI and the Annex 3 summary, and the reverse stress on the Chilean legal minimum stack.

## Expected results

The ranges are in the manifest (`expect`) and were written before the bake: the headline capital between 2 and 30 percent of RWA, the Gaussian copula figure between 40 and 100 percent of the simple sum, the CET1 ratio at the end of the Fed severe path between -20 and 11 percent, the PE ratio at the jump-off between 10 and 25 percent, and the reverse stress distance between 0.1 and 12.

## Reading the results

* The headline is the simple sum. The Gaussian copula figure sits well below it and the worst case above it: the band is the dependence uncertainty, and a diversified number is never capital (dossier 05 l.519).
* Only credit responds to the scenario through the satellites, and the net interest income of the PPNR row follows the rate path of each variant. Market (no position book) and operational (no source maps a macro path to it) losses carry their expected loss, so the stressed loss is a floor; an EVE change is not a profit-and-loss item and stays out of the capital path.
* The loss-only path credits no earnings, so even the baseline ends below the CET1 stack; the PPNR row shows the other reading, with unsourced fee and cost ratios.
* The satellites predict a 2020 peak that did not happen (the stimulus of 2020 held the charge-offs down). The stress losses of the scenarios therefore rest on models that failed their out-of-time test; the sign test is red as well.
* The EBA annual averages are read as year-end levels and the IRRBB correlations are a convention (UNVERIFIED); the t-copula and correlation stress rows show how much each moves the figure.

## Limits

* US-calibrated satellites applied to a Chilean bank (decision Q6); the Chilean satellites are backlog item BL-077.
* The AT1/T2 split of the effective equity is in no source (an UNVERIFIED assumption): the part above CET1 is carried as T2 and AT1 is nil, which makes Tier 1 equal to CET1.
* No mitigation is assumed (EBA para 33), so row 10.2 of the Annex 3 is N/A.
* The reverse stress runs on the labelled PPNR path, because the loss-only baseline already ends below the stack; it rests on the assumptions of the PPNR row (decision Z10).
* The stressed credit path rests on satellites that fail their own 2020 to 2025 back-cast.
* The credit marginal uses representative names per segment, not the 171,500 accounts: 400 and 1,600 were measured with the same seed and 400 are used (the models document records the difference and the standard error).
