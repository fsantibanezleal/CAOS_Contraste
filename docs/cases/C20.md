# Case C20, ML model validation: drift, robustness, explanation stability, leakage

- **Category:** `ml-ai-risk`
- **Source:** real labels with known synthetic shifts (`synthetic-known-truth` for the shifted variants, `real-outcomes` for the seed sweep): the
  SantanderAI Stressed German Credit Dataset SGCD-v0.1 (source `santanderai-sgcd`, data CC BY 4.0, commit `8403fa9`), the UCI Taiwan and Polish
  files (CC BY 4.0) and the three Federal Reserve 2025 hypothetical corporate portfolios (public domain by the Board's statement). Methods:
  `riskvalidation.ml`, `riskvalidation.fairness` and `riskvalidation.validation` (engine contracts `engine-ml-api` and `engine-fairness-api`,
  pinned by the commit in `data-pipeline/requirements.txt`). Code: `data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c20/`; documents: `data/derived/C20/`; contracts
  `c20-outputs`, `c20-outputs-shift`, `c20-outputs-seeds`, `c20-outputs-perturbation`, `c20-outputs-leakage` and `c20-models` 1.0.0.
- **One line:** whether performance, drift, attribution and reason-code stability survive shocks a model has and has not seen, shifted
  populations, input noise and retraining seeds, and whether the leakage screen catches a planted leak. The SGCD data must not be used for
  real-world credit scoring.

## Executive brief

**Initiative.** The head of model risk management, with the validator of the machine-learning credit models, owns the evidence that a
learned model keeps its performance and its explanations when the world moves: a shock in the data feed, a shifted population, noisy inputs, a
retraining with another seed, or a feature that was never available at decision time. Dossier 06 records the supervisory text that asks for it:
the ECB guide to internal models on the randomness of the training process and on stored seeds (paragraphs 43 and 59), the EBA report on
machine learning for IRB models, and the EU AI Act, whose high-risk obligations for credit scoring (Annex III 5(b)) apply from 2027-12-02. The
reason to act now is that a validator who only re-measures the AUC on a holdout sees none of the four failures below.

**Outcomes.**

| Outcome | Proof point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A model that is stable across seeds can still be fragile to input noise | the AUC standard deviation across 20 seeds is 0.0011, yet the AUC falls 0.071 AUC points at noise 0.1 (tolerance 0.02) and keeps falling to 0.126 at noise 1; with the exact zeros kept at zero the loss at 0.1 is 0.008 | `outputs.rows` of `perturbation-grid.json` and its control row in `tests` |
| The reasons given to an applicant are less stable than the score | the top-four reason overlap across 100 bootstrap refits is 0.73 on average and 0.53 at the 5th percentile, against a seed-to-seed AUC spread of 0.0011 | `outputs.rows[].reason_code_overlap` of `seed-sweep.json` |
| A shifted population breaks the coverage promise of a conformal set, with no label needed to see it | the 90 percent sets cover 89.5 percent at the baseline and 71.0 to 88.0 percent under the three Fed-sized shifts (80.9 percent pooled, p below 0.0001), while CBPE estimates the AUC without labels at 0.848 against 0.691 measured | `synthetic_shift.label_free` of `fed-shift.json` |
| A planted leak is caught by the screen, and the performance it bought disappears at decision time | the single-feature AUC of the planted column is 0.9998 and 9,000 cells are dated after the observation date; the model scores 0.9997 with the leak and 0.503 without it, while the clean control passes every screen | `screen` and `performance` of `leakage-planted.json` |
| A structured-only model cannot see most shipped shocks, a two-view check can | the shipped F1 shock reaches 16 of 30 records of the structured view and F2 and F4 reach none, while the consistency check of the structured and the text view finds 20 percent of the checked fields contradicting in the held-out F4 rows | `shipped.rows` of `sgcd-seen.json` and `sgcd-held-out.json` |

**Positioning.** The case does not say that any model is good or bad. It reproduces on public benchmark data the tests an ML validator runs,
with the engine of Contraste, and shows how far each answer moves when the shock, the shift, the noise, the seed or the leak moves. No result
is a compliance statement.

**Credibility.** The labels are the UCI files and the SGCD records, the shifts are known by construction, and every statistic and test is
computed by `riskvalidation`; the documents pass the schema, `check_c20` and the schema of every result row before they are written. The
unknowns are named: SGCD is a synthetic benchmark that its card forbids using for real credit scoring, the Fed portfolios carry no outcome,
the shift sizes and the cut-offs are conventions, and the Polish files carry no date.

## Why this case exists

A learned credit model is validated on more than its accuracy. A validator asks whether the answer holds when the input feed changes (drift
and data quality), when the population moves (shift and coverage), when a value is noisy (robustness), when the training is repeated (seeds and
reason codes) and whether the model leaned on something it should not have (leakage). C20 puts those questions to the boosted-tree rungs of the
product's own ladder, on benchmarks where the shift or the leak is known, so that a test can be shown to find what was planted, and where a
fairness battery on the Taiwan model rides beside the answers, labelled.

## Data and what is real

The raw files live in the device data root (`$CONTRASTE_DATA`), never in the repository: the SGCD files and the Fed portfolios are fetched by
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c20/fetch.py` with a provenance record (URL, size, SHA-256, retrieval date) and listed in
`data/derived/C20/raw-sources.txt`; the UCI files are pinned in `data/sources/manifest.json`.

| Dataset | Rows | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| SGCD-v0.1 (20 batches: S1 to S4 setup, A1 to A10 adaptation, T1 to T6 test) | 1,000 | the German model is fitted on the 700 setup and adaptation records and scored on the 300 locked test records; the shipped shocks and Contraste's own application of each family |
| UCI Taiwan default of credit card clients | 30,000 | model of the seed, perturbation and leakage variants; 9,000 locked holdout rows |
| UCI Polish companies bankruptcy, 3-year file | 10,503 | model of the Fed shift probes; 3,151 locked holdout rows |
| Federal Reserve 2025 hypothetical corporate portfolios (typical, higher risk, lower risk) | 3 x 200 | real drift between the portfolios and the size of a synthetic shift |

Every SGCD record is a UCI German applicant (930 of the 930 complete records match a UCI row), so the German model is never fitted on the UCI
file as a whole. A model that reads the structured view alone is reached by the shipped shocks through F1 only (U14 research addendum H4); the
second block therefore applies each family to the structured view with the metadata's probabilities, fields and seed 42. What is a convention,
labelled so in the documents and in `docs/design/features/c20/design.md`: the nine named parameters (decision V6), the way each family acts on
the structured view, the bins of the drift (ten for the PSI, five for the CSI), the size of the synthetic shift (a mean displacement of the five
leading features, solved to a multiple of the Fed rating drift), the windows of the conformal coverage, the leak and its date, the cut-off that
declines the worst 20 percent of the baseline decisions, and the age bands of the fairness battery.

## Formalization

Per variant, on a locked holdout, with shifts on the evaluation set only:

1. SGCD seen (T1 to T3, F1 and F2) and held out (T4 to T6, F4): performance, the PSI and CSI of each input against the setup batches, the
   chi-square p-value, the completeness and the consistency of the two views, and the rank correlation of the mean absolute TreeSHAP attribution
   against the clean rows; the decision-flip share at the fixed cut-off for each applied shock.
2. Fed shift probes: the engine's PSI, CSI, KS and chi-square between the three portfolios (no model), and the Polish model under the shifts
   sized by the Fed rating drift, with the worst-10-percent resilience test against a development-only permutation reference (199 draws), split
   conformal coverage against 1 - alpha by window and the CBPE estimate, always an estimate.
3. Seed sweep: the LightGBM and XGBoost rungs refitted with 20 seeds (AUC dispersion, grades changed, Kendall rank stability of the
   attributions), the reason codes (four largest signed TreeSHAP contributions toward default) across 100 bootstrap refits, and the rank
   agreement of the attributions with the range of the accumulated local effects.
4. Perturbation grid: the engine's Gaussian noise at 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 and 1 standard deviation on the evaluation features, the finding at
   the preset scale 0.1 (loss above 0.02 AUC points), the overfit gap and the weak spots, and a control that keeps every exact zero at zero.
5. Planted leakage: a feature built from the outcome and dated after the observation date, the four screens, the clean control and the AUC at
   decision time.
6. The labelled fairness battery (equalized odds, equal opportunity, calibration within groups, AUC by group; sex, age band and marital
   status) on the Taiwan model of each Taiwan variant.

## Expected results

Declared before the bake and wide on purpose: the AUC of the LightGBM rung on the Taiwan holdout lies between 0.74 and 0.82, on the German
SGCD test batches between 0.62 and 0.86 and on the Polish holdout between 0.80 and 0.97; the AUC with the planted leak is at least 0.97 and the
leak is caught; the AUC standard deviation across seeds is below 0.01; the Fed rating PSI between the typical and the higher-risk portfolio lies
between 0.3 and 3; the baseline conformal coverage lies between 0.85 and 0.95. A result outside is a finding to read, not a range to tune.

## Reading the results

An amber or red light says an indicator crossed a convention of the engine's policy; none is a regulatory threshold and none is a finding of a
defect of any lender. A small shock row (27 to 90 records on the seen shocks, 60 to 90 on the held-out ones) has a wide interval on its AUC: read the direction of the change, not its size, and read
the shipped F2 change of the seen document (a shock that does not reach the structured view) as the noise floor of a 30-record row. The
perturbation loss at small noise comes from the exact zeros of the repayment status and the payments, which the boosted trees read as a bin of
their own; with the zeros kept the loss is an order of magnitude smaller, so the finding is about how the model reads a zero and not about
sensitivity to small changes in general. The conformal windows are shifts, not time: a coverage loss is read as a broken exchangeability, never
as proof of a defect. The CBPE estimate assumes calibrated probabilities, no concept drift and no extrapolation; where a shift breaks the last
two it can be far from the measured AUC, as the Fed-sized shifts show.

## Validation anchor

Every number of a document equals a `riskvalidation` call on the arrays the document's variant hands it. The planted shifts and the planted leak are
the known truths: the shifts are sized by construction (the PSI of the score reaches 0.25, 0.5 and 1 times the Fed rating drift to 0.1 percent),
the leak is caught by two of the four screens and the clean control by none, and the Shapley additivity of the LightGBM rung holds to 1e-14. The
documents pass `tests/contracts_u14/semantics.py::check_c20` and the schema of every result row; the engine's own size and power are in the engine
repository (decision Q12).

## Limits

- SGCD must not be used for real-world credit scoring (its card says so); nothing here scores a person, and its shock rows hold 27 to 90 records.
- The Fed portfolios carry no default outcome and no decision. They show real drift between portfolios and fix the size of a synthetic shift;
  no model is scored on them, and they cannot pass contract 1 as it stands (open question in the report).
- The shifts are synthetic: a known mean displacement of five features with the labels unchanged, so the concept is constant by construction.
- The Polish and Taiwan files carry no date or a single snapshot: the windows of the coverage are shifts, and the holdout stands in for the
  out-of-time sample of the resemblance screen. The Polish reader is the minimal one C20 needs until the one of `cases/c02/` lands.
- Only the two boosted-tree rungs are swept (the other rungs are deterministic given the data or have no TreeSHAP attribution), the rounds
  are the first fit's, and XGBoost computes in single precision, so its Shapley additivity gap is near 1e-6 and the engine's 1e-9 tolerance
  flags it.
- The fairness battery is descriptive evidence on one benchmark model; the four-fifths ratio is an employment benchmark and is not part of C20.
- `engine-ml-api` on this branch is 1.0.0 and the engine at the pinned commit is binding where they differ (decision G18).
