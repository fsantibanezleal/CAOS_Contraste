# Requirements: U14-P1, case C02 (corporate PD across the 1 to 5 year horizons on the UCI Polish bankruptcy files)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c02/`; the documents are `data/derived/C02/` (contracts `c02-outputs` and `c02-models` 1.0.0, the producer
side); the methods are `riskvalidation` (`validation.discrimination`, `validation.calibration`, `validation.stability`, `ml`,
`validation.ml`, `provisions`, `validation.ifrs9`; engine contracts `engine-ml-api` and `engine-provisions-api`, the consumer side).
The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md). The files carry no date: no
out-of-time claim is made anywhere.
