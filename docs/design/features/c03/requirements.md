# Requirements: U6-P1, case C03 (SME PD by vintage through 2008, on the SBA 7(a) loans)

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-801 | WHEN the C03 bake reads the SBA 7(a) FOIA files through the shared reader `pipeline/io/sba.py`, THE panel SHALL keep one typed row per disbursed loan with no borrower name, address or identifier, SHALL count every row left out by its reason (nothing dropped silently), and THE model inputs SHALL be origination attributes only (amount, guaranteed share, term, sector, revolving), never an outcome or a date after approval. | `tests/c03/test_panel.py::test_the_panel_counts_every_exclusion_and_the_inputs_are_origination_attributes` |
| CT-802 | THE target of a loan SHALL be a charge-off dated within 60 months of its approval and on or before the observation date; WHEN the observation date is on or after the 60-month horizon end and no charge-off is dated inside it, THE loan SHALL be a non-event (the file shows a charge-off as CHGOFF, never as active); WHEN the observation ends before the horizon end and the loan has not charged off, THE loan SHALL carry no outcome (censored) and SHALL NOT enter a rate or a rung as a performing loan. | `tests/c03/test_target.py::test_the_label_is_an_event_a_non_event_or_censored_by_the_observation_date` |
| CT-803 | EACH test cohort SHALL train only on approval fiscal years whose 5-year mark falls before the first approval date of the cohort (decision D1: `last_fy <= first_fy - 6`, cutoff 30 September of `first_fy - 1`), the training windows of FY2006-07 and FY2008-10 SHALL end in FY2000 and FY2002, THE crisis flag SHALL be true only where the window holds a charge-off dated in the 2007-12 to 2009-06 recession, and a window that overlaps its cohort SHALL be refused. | `tests/c03/test_windows.py::test_each_window_closes_its_five_year_marks_before_the_first_approval_of_its_cohort` |
| CT-804 | A model for a test cohort SHALL see only loans approved on or before the cutoff of its window and, of each, only what happened on or before it: perturbing every approval and every outcome after the cutoff SHALL leave the training labels, the declared directions and every fitted rung (its predictions on fixed inputs) bit-identical. | `tests/c03/test_cutoff.py::test_a_model_for_a_cohort_ignores_everything_after_its_cutoff` |
| CT-805 | THE 5-year observed PD of a vintage SHALL be a number only when its 5-year mark (30 September of the fiscal year plus 5) is on or before the files' as-of date, and otherwise SHALL be null with its reason in the vintage row, its summary, its calibration inputs and its stability row; a vintage curve SHALL hold observed values only up to the months the vintage has been observed. | `tests/c03/test_target.py::test_a_vintage_before_its_five_year_mark_carries_no_observed_value` |
| CT-806 | THE C03 bake SHALL fit the existing rungs of the C01 ladder (constant, WoE scorecard, penalised logit, PLTR, EBM, monotone gradient boosting) on each training window, with the monotone directions declared from the training window only, SHALL declare the TabPFN challenger absent with its reason, and WHEN a window holds fewer events or non-events than a rung needs THE rung SHALL be reported as not evaluated with the counts, never fitted. | `tests/c03/test_ladder.py::test_a_thin_window_is_reported_with_its_counts_and_not_fitted` |
| CT-807 | EVERY validation row of a C03 document SHALL be a `riskvalidation` 0.5.0 `TestResult` run under the engine's policy (a row that cannot run is `not_evaluated` with null numbers and a reason), and THE C03 package SHALL define no PD or validation function of the engine. | `tests/c03/test_engine_calls.py::test_the_product_defines_no_engine_function` |
| CT-808 | EVERY document under `data/derived/C03/` SHALL validate against `c03-outputs` or `c03-models`, THE manifest SHALL list each document with its path and exact size, no document SHALL carry a loan row or a loan field, and THE documents SHALL state their amounts in USD. | `tests/c03/test_documents.py::test_every_document_validates_and_the_manifest_lists_it` |
| CT-809 | WHEN the bake runs twice with the same seed and data, THE two sets of documents SHALL have identical content hashes. | `tests/c03/test_documents.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_give_identical_hashes` |
| CT-810 | THE five variants SHALL be the test cohorts FY2006-07, 2008-10, 2011-14, 2015-17 and 2018-20 in that order, each with its own training window in the models document equal to the window of its variant, and the PD of every rung SHALL be open (0, 1) at every estimate site. | `tests/c03/test_documents.py::test_the_five_variants_are_in_order_and_each_carries_its_own_window` |
| CT-811 | WHEN the bake declares an expected range for a result, THE bake SHALL fail naming a result outside it. | `tests/c03/test_documents.py::test_an_expected_range_is_enforced` |
| CT-812 | THE case page SHALL state that the data are SBA 7(a) small-business loans and not a bank's SME book, the right-censoring of the loans still active, and the training window of each variant; its result table SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents, and every window it states SHALL equal the window of the documents. | `tests/c03/test_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_states_its_data_and_its_results_come_from_the_documents` |
