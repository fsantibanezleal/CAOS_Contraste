# Requirements: U4, case C04 (rating transitions and TTC PD by grade)

Status: planned

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-401 | THE CEREP fetcher SHALL fetch, for S&P's, Moody's and Fitch's EU entities (corporate, long-term, categories), the default-rate page (tab 2), the defaults page (tab 3) and the transition page (tab 4) of every calendar-year cohort from 2000 to 2025 and the transition page of every semester from 2010 to 2025, into the device data root, at least four seconds between requests, retrying with backoff, never refetching a file already present unless asked, and recording every file's hash in the licence manifest. | `tests/test_cerep.py::test_fetch_plan_paced_and_cached` |
| CT-402 | THE CEREP reader SHALL map each agency's labels to one scale of seven performing grades (AAA, AA, A, BBB, BB, B, CCC to C and Moody's equivalents), a default state (S&P's R, SD and D; Fitch's RD and D; none on Moody's transition page) and withdrawals (the Withdrawals column with WD, WR and NR at the end), SHALL refuse a label it does not know, and SHALL give for every cohort the count matrix with its withdrawals, the distinct defaulted ratings by grade (tab 2) and the default events by grade (tab 3), with tab 2's rates taken over tab 2's own cohort (tab 4's row where it reproduces the printed rate within its two-decimal rounding, the count over the printed rate otherwise), every gap between the two cohorts recorded and a gap above 50% refused as a misread page. | `tests/test_cerep.py::test_reader_maps_every_agency` |
| CT-403 | FOR each agency, THE C04 variant SHALL report the one-year PD by grade and cohort under three definitions (distinct defaulted ratings over the cohort, tab 2; default events over the cohort, tab 3; the transition page's default column over the cohort less its withdrawals, tab 4, where the scale has a default category), the long-run average default rate by grade (EBA/GL/2017/16 paragraph 84) with Wald, Agresti-Coull and Jeffreys intervals, and the gap between the definitions. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_pd_definitions` |
| CT-404 | FOR each agency, THE C04 variant SHALL report the pooled one-year matrix (Anderson and Goodman (2.8)), its embedding diagnostics (S and the Theorem 3 conditions), the diagonal, weighted, JLT and EM generators with their L1 distances and one-year PDs by grade, M_SVD and the trace index by cohort, the time-homogeneity test across cohorts and across semesters, the reference test of every cohort against the pooled matrix, and the product of two semester matrices against the annual one. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_agency_statistics` |
| CT-405 | THE known-truth variants SHALL draw from riskvalidation's `RatingPaths` with a generator estimated by EM on S&P's pooled counts and cohort sizes like S&P's, and SHALL report over their repetitions, each rate with its Monte Carlo standard error: the Markov variant, the error of the cohort, duration, EM, diagonal and weighted PDs by grade against the truth, the share of zero estimates, the size of the four transition tests and the coverage of the Wald, Agresti-Coull, Jeffreys and bootstrap intervals; the momentum, cycle and withdrawal variants, the rejection rates of the tests and the bias of the PDs by grade along their ladders, with the error of the Markov projection at five years under momentum; the thin variant, coverage and the separation of adjacent grades by cohort size. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_generator_families` |
| CT-406 | THE published variant SHALL carry Israel, Rosenthal and Wei's nine distances, Schuermann and Hanson's Table 5 and Engelmann's section 4, recomputed by riskvalidation from the papers in the data root, each beside its printed value, without the agency matrices the papers reprint. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_published_answers` |
| CT-407 | EVERY CEREP-derived table SHALL carry the attribution "Source: ESMA CEREP; tables transformed by Contraste" and the agency's entity, scope and period, and every PD shown SHALL name its default definition, with ESMA's statement that no common definition of a default event is set for CEREP. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_attribution_and_definitions` |
| CT-408 | A C04 finding SHALL be evidenced by values of its artifact, and the bake SHALL fail if a declared expected range does not hold, naming the result. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_findings_and_ranges` |
| CT-409 | EVERY C04 variant SHALL carry its truth status (real outcomes, synthetic known truth or published answer) and its lineage, and two bakes from the same raw files and seed SHALL give identical artifacts. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_lineage_and_determinism` |
| CT-410 | THE web SHALL project live a reader's portfolio by grade under an agency's pooled matrix and an origination mix (Engelmann's propagation (9)), with the TTC portfolio and the default rate by year, and the TypeScript results SHALL equal riskvalidation's on the exported parity points within 1e-9 relative. | `frontend/src/engine/transitions.test.ts` |
| CT-411 | THE web SHALL compute live the Wald and Agresti-Coull intervals with the dependence correction (3.4) at a reader's default correlation, and the IRB capital of a portfolio by grade under each PD definition, equal to riskvalidation's on the parity points within 1e-9 relative. | `frontend/src/engine/transitions.test.ts` |
| CT-412 | THE C04 instrument SHALL keep the six groups in order, show the lane and the provenance on every view, hold a live read-out in each rail section that changes with its controls, and show views that fit the variant (agency, generator family or papers). | `frontend/src/workbench/c04.test.tsx` |
| CT-413 | WHEN the measured gate walks C04 at five sizes, both themes and both languages, THE site SHALL pass every check. | `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G1 to G9) |
| CT-415 | FOR each agency and five-year window (2000 to 2004 through 2020 to 2024), THE C04 variant SHALL report the observed five-year outcome of the window's fixed cohort by grade (the shares in default and withdrawn at the end, tab 4; the cumulative default rate, tab 2) beside the projections chained from one-year matrices (the window's own annual matrices with withdrawals kept as a state and removed; the pooled matrix to the fifth power; the EM generator at five years), the lifetime PD a matrix gives against the one the window's cohort lived. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_lifetime_check` |
| CT-414 | THE docs SHALL hold the case page with its data, definitions, methods, results and caveats, linked from the case index, and the case content in the web SHALL cite every source it uses. | `tests/test_c04.py::test_case_page`, `scripts/check_doc_paths.py` |
