# Requirements: U5-P1, case C06 (lifetime PD, IFRS 9 staging and ECL on the SBA 7(a) twin)

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-501 | WHEN the C06 bake reads the SBA 7(a) FOIA files, THE reader SHALL return one typed row per loan with no borrower name, address or identifier column, THE cleaning SHALL count every row left out by its reason (nothing dropped silently), and the book it builds SHALL satisfy the `loan_panel` contract of contract 1 with no rejected row. | `tests/c06/test_c06_readers.py::test_sba_reader_feeds_the_loan_panel_contract` |
| CT-502 | WHEN the C06 bake reads the Federal Reserve 2026 tables, THE reader SHALL return the history and the two supervisory scenarios, THE macro paths the model stands on SHALL satisfy the `macro_path` contract with no value whose vintage date is after the reporting date, and a Federal Reserve or SBA file that is absent SHALL stop the bake with the URL it is fetched from. | `tests/c06/test_c06_readers.py::test_a_missing_input_stops_the_bake_with_its_url` |
| CT-503 | WHERE the owner's Freddie Mac account is missing, THE Freddie Mac reader slot SHALL raise a declared absence, and every variant SHALL state that Freddie Mac data is not available with that reason and run on the SBA twin. | `tests/c06/test_c06_readers.py::test_the_freddie_mac_slot_declares_its_absence` |
| CT-504 | A model for a reporting date SHALL see only the loans approved on or before the date and, of each loan, only the exits and the macro values on or before it: perturbing every outcome, approval and macro value after the date SHALL leave the fitted rungs, the staging and the ECL of that date bit-identical. | `tests/c06/test_c06_cutoff.py::test_a_model_for_a_date_ignores_everything_after_it` |
| CT-505 | WHEN a loan has no default or payment by the reporting date, THE panel SHALL censor it at the date (or at its maturity) and the hazard cells SHALL count its exposure only for the quarters it was observed, never as performing to its term. | `tests/c06/test_c06_cutoff.py::test_open_loans_are_censored_not_performing_to_term` |
| CT-506 | THE C06 bake SHALL fit three rungs at each reporting date (Kaplan-Meier, a discrete-time hazard with macro covariates, Cox proportional hazards) on statsmodels, and WHEN a vintage holds fewer loans or defaults than a fit needs THE rung SHALL be reported with its counts as not evaluated with the reason, never fitted. | `tests/c06/test_c06_ladder.py::test_a_thin_vintage_is_reported_with_its_count_and_not_fitted` |
| CT-507 | EVERY term structure, stage, ECL, probability weight and allowance of C06 SHALL be computed by the pinned `riskvalidation` 0.5.0 (tag v0.05.000): the engine's golden values SHALL reproduce through the pin, the product SHALL define none of its functions, and each term structure of a document SHALL satisfy the survival identities. | `tests/c06/test_c06_engine_calls.py::test_the_engine_golden_values_reproduce_through_the_pin` |
| CT-508 | THE staging SHALL be the engine's `allocate_stages` with the policy of the document, the file having no delinquency data SHALL show as no backstop, forbearance or watch-list trigger, and the policy of the variant SHALL equal the policy of its models document field by field. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_staging_uses_the_policy_and_no_delinquency_trigger_fires` |
| CT-509 | THE model ECL SHALL be probability-weighted over at least two labelled scenarios, each scenario's figure SHALL be labelled, and an overlay SHALL be its own waterfall line carried in its stage and SHALL NOT change the model ECL. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_overlays_are_separate_lines_and_never_change_the_model_ecl` |
| CT-510 | THE allowance and gross carrying amount roll-forwards SHALL close by stage, the waterfall SHALL open into them, and the engine's `check_identities` SHALL return no failing identity for every committed document. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_reconciliation_closes_and_the_waterfall_opens_into_it` |
| CT-511 | EACH variant SHALL carry the IFRS 9 battery of the engine (lifetime PD backtest by cohort and rung, SICR effectiveness, threshold stability, stage migration, ECL backtest, scenario sensitivity, non-linearity, overlay diagnostics, reconciliation identities) as rows of `test-result`, with a lifetime backtest by origination cohort whose unobserved horizon is null with its reason. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_the_battery_rows_are_engine_test_results` |
| CT-512 | EVERY document under `data/derived/C06/` SHALL validate against its schema, THE manifest SHALL list each document with its path and size, no document SHALL carry a loan row, and a sample of accounts SHALL carry no identifier. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_every_document_validates_and_the_manifest_lists_it` |
| CT-513 | WHEN the bake runs twice with the same seed, THE two sets of documents SHALL have identical content hashes. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_give_identical_hashes` |
| CT-514 | THE case page SHALL say that the data are small-business loans and not mortgages, that Freddie Mac's data is not used and why, SHALL NOT claim a Freddie Mac or mortgage result, and its result table SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c06/test_c06_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_states_its_data_and_its_results_come_from_the_documents` |
| CT-515 | THE C06 package SHALL hold no ECL, staging, term-structure or validation formula: each engine function it uses SHALL be imported from `riskvalidation` and none SHALL be redefined. | `tests/c06/test_c06_engine_calls.py::test_the_product_defines_no_engine_function` |
| CT-516 | THE six variants SHALL be the reporting dates 2006-12, 2008-12, 2010-12, 2019-12, 2020-06 and 2023-06 in that order, each with its models document, and the models of a date SHALL be trained up to its cutoff (the training cutoff of each rung is the reporting date). | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_the_six_variants_are_in_order_and_trained_to_their_cutoff` |
| CT-517 | EVERY amount of a C06 document SHALL be in USD with its unit stated, and WHEN the bake declares an expected range for a result, THE bake SHALL fail naming a result outside it. | `tests/c06/test_c06_documents.py::test_amounts_are_in_usd_and_an_expected_range_is_enforced` |
