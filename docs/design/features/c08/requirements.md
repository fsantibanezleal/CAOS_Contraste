# Requirements: U6-P2, case C08 (LGD and downturn LGD on the SBA 7(a) charge-off twin)

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The IDs of this unit run from 901 to 917,
clear of the ranges the other cases use.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-901 | WHEN the C08 bake reads the SBA 7(a) FOIA files, THE cleaning SHALL count every charge-off left out by its reason (no charge-off date, no valid amount or approval, an implausible term, a charge-off before the approval, a proxy ratio above ten, a charge-off after the last cohort), nothing SHALL be dropped silently, and the borrower's name, address and identifiers SHALL never be read. | `tests/c08/test_c08_panel.py::test_cleaning_counts_every_exclusion_under_its_reason` |
| CT-902 | THE LGD proxy of a charged-off loan SHALL be `GrossChargeOffAmount` over `GrossApproval` (decision D2 of the U6 sizing), every C08 document SHALL state the proxy, its formula, that the file has no recovery cash flows and that the bias is downward (the approved amount overstates the exposure at default of an amortising loan), and `exposure_field` SHALL be `GrossApproval`. | `tests/c08/test_c08_panel.py::test_the_proxy_is_the_gross_charge_off_over_the_gross_approval` |
| CT-903 | A realised LGD below 0 or above 1 SHALL be carried with its range flag and counted in the distribution and the cohort mean, SHALL never be clipped, and the training set of a rung SHALL leave such a value out by a counted exclusion (the engine fits take LGD in [0, 1]), never by clipping it to the end of the range. | `tests/c08/test_c08_panel.py::test_an_lgd_outside_zero_one_is_flagged_and_never_clipped` |
| CT-904 | A model for a cutoff SHALL be trained only on defaults whose charge-off closed strictly before the cutoff (the engine's `closed_before`): perturbing every default charged off on or after the cutoff SHALL leave the fitted coefficients, the predictions and the training counts of that cutoff bit-identical. | `tests/c08/test_c08_cutoff.py::test_a_model_for_a_cutoff_ignores_every_default_after_it` |
| CT-905 | THE training block SHALL state the cutoff, the number of defaults closed before it and the number of open workouts excluded (the charge-offs dated in the twelve months after the cutoff, the nearest stand-in for a workout open at the cutoff since the file has no default date), and no open workout SHALL enter the training set. | `tests/c08/test_c08_cutoff.py::test_open_workouts_at_the_cutoff_are_excluded_and_counted` |
| CT-906 | THE variants SHALL be the default cohorts 2003-06, 2007-08, 2009-10, 2011-13, 2014-19 and 2020-23 in that order by default year, each with its models document, each trained up to the day before its first default year and tested on the cohort (out of time by default date), and the realised against predicted rows SHALL cover only the test cohort and later ones. | `tests/c08/test_c08_documents.py::test_the_six_cohorts_are_in_order_and_each_variant_is_out_of_time` |
| CT-907 | THE C08 bake SHALL fit the OLS, fractional logit, beta, hurdle, tobit and gradient-boosted rungs (the engine for the first five, catboost for the last), every prediction SHALL lie in [0, 1], the beta rung SHALL be reported at no fewer than two epsilons, and WHEN a rung cannot be fitted THE rung SHALL be reported as not evaluated with its reason and no number, never a stand-in value. | `tests/c08/test_c08_ladder.py::test_each_rung_predicts_inside_zero_one_and_beta_reports_its_epsilons` |
| CT-908 | EVERY LGD fit, floor, downturn function and validation test of C08 SHALL be computed by the pinned `riskvalidation` 0.6.0 (tag v0.06.000): the golden values of `engine-lgd-ead-api` SHALL reproduce through the pin. | `tests/c08/test_c08_engine_calls.py::test_the_engine_golden_values_reproduce_through_the_pin` |
| CT-909 | THE C08 package SHALL hold no LGD fit, floor, downturn or test statistic of its own: each engine function it uses SHALL be imported from `riskvalidation` and none SHALL be redefined. | `tests/c08/test_c08_engine_calls.py::test_the_product_defines_no_engine_function` |
| CT-910 | EACH variant SHALL carry the calibration by segment and over time and the discrimination of every fitted rung and the downturn coverage of every downturn cohort as rows of `test-result` written by the engine, and a not evaluated row SHALL carry null statistic, p value and metric and a reason. | `tests/c08/test_c08_documents.py::test_the_tests_are_engine_test_results_and_a_rung_that_is_not_fitted_is_not_evaluated` |
| CT-911 | THE downturn period SHALL be identified by the engine's `identify_downturn` from the supplied annual charge-off rate of the SBA book, THE add-on SHALL use only the cohorts inside the downturn period, and each row's add-on SHALL equal its downturn LGD minus its long-run LGD, covered and shortfall being the engine's `coverage_shortfall`. | `tests/c08/test_c08_downturn_floors.py::test_the_add_on_uses_only_the_downturn_cohorts` |
| CT-912 | THE floors bite SHALL be the engine's `apply_lgd_floor` on the reference rung's predictions of the test cohort, each segment SHALL name one of the 12 transcribed floor cells (the collateral is inferred from the term and the revolving status, stated as such), and an uplift SHALL never be negative. | `tests/c08/test_c08_downturn_floors.py::test_the_floor_bite_is_the_engines_and_the_segments_map_to_the_cells` |
| CT-913 | WHERE the owner's Freddie Mac account is missing, THE Freddie Mac reader slot SHALL raise a declared absence and every C08 document SHALL state that Freddie Mac data is not available with that reason and no number SHALL stand in for it. | `tests/c08/test_c08_documents.py::test_every_document_declares_the_freddie_mac_slot_absent` |
| CT-914 | EVERY document under `data/derived/C08/` SHALL validate against `c08-outputs` or `c08-models`, THE manifest SHALL list each document with its path and size, and no document SHALL carry a loan row or a borrower field. | `tests/c08/test_c08_documents.py::test_every_document_validates_and_the_manifest_lists_it` |
| CT-915 | WHEN the bake runs twice with the same seed, THE two sets of documents SHALL have identical content hashes. | `tests/c08/test_c08_documents.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_give_identical_hashes` |
| CT-916 | THE case page SHALL state the proxy, its bias, that the Freddie Mac slot is absent and the limits, SHALL NOT claim a Freddie Mac or mortgage result, and its result table SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c08/test_c08_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_states_its_proxy_and_its_results_come_from_the_documents` |
| CT-917 | EVERY amount of a C08 document SHALL be in USD with its unit stated, and WHEN the bake declares an expected range for a result, THE bake SHALL fail naming a result outside it. | `tests/c08/test_c08_documents.py::test_amounts_are_in_usd_and_an_expected_range_is_enforced` |
