# Requirements: U7-P, case C10 (portfolio credit capital)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The
bake is `data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c10/`; the documents are `data/derived/C10/` (contracts `c10-outputs`, `c10-outputs-grid`
and `c10-models` 1.0.0, the producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.portfolio` and `riskvalidation.validation.portfolio`
(engine contract `engine-portfolio-api` 1.0.0, the consumer side, pinned by commit 502652b of `task/u7-portfolio` until the
integrator tags the release). The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md).

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-1101 | THE Federal Reserve reader SHALL name every column of the Board's charge-off table by the header ids of its cells and never by position, SHALL turn each percentage into a decimal rate, SHALL keep a quarter the Board marks n.a. as a missing quarter and SHALL refuse a cell that is neither a rate nor n.a. | `tests/c10/test_c10_reader.py::test_the_reader_names_columns_by_header_id_and_keeps_missing_quarters_missing` |
| CT-1102 | THE annual loss rate of a year SHALL be the mean of its four quarterly rates, a year with a missing quarter SHALL carry a null rate and its reason and SHALL NOT be interpolated, and `data_status.missing_quarters` SHALL list the years that lack a quarter. | `tests/c10/test_c10_reader.py::test_the_annual_rate_is_the_mean_of_four_quarters_and_a_partial_year_is_null_with_its_reason` |
| CT-1103 | THE fit window SHALL be 1985-2006 and the evaluation window 2007-2012, and no evaluation-window year SHALL enter the fit: the fit SHALL be identical when the evaluation rates are changed. | `tests/c10/test_c10_windows.py::test_the_fit_is_the_same_when_the_evaluation_years_change` |
