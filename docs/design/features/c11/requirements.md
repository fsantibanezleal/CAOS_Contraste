# Requirements: U8-P1, case C11 (market VaR and ES back-testing across 2008, 2020, 2022 and 2023)

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-601 | WHEN the C11 bake reads the Kenneth French, US Treasury and Federal Reserve H.10 files, THE readers SHALL return typed daily series of the observed days with every raw file described by its URL, size, hash and retrieval date, THE three series SHALL satisfy the `market_series` family of contract 1 with no rejected row, and a raw file that is absent SHALL stop the bake naming the URL it is fetched from. | `tests/c11/test_c11_readers.py::test_the_three_series_pass_contract_one`, `tests/c11/test_c11_readers.py::test_fetch_records_url_size_hash_and_date`, `tests/test_io_treasury.py::test_series_view_lists_an_empty_cell_as_missing_and_never_fills_it` |
| CT-602 | WHEN a day is missing from a public series (an empty cell, ND or the French missing code) or from one component of a book, THE day SHALL be skipped in the rolling window and counted in `data_status.missing_days`, and SHALL never be filled. | `tests/c11/test_c11_readers.py::test_adversarial_a_missing_day_is_counted_and_never_filled` |
| CT-603 | THE six windows SHALL have the forecast dates of decision D2 and the portfolios stated in the case page (US equity, the 10-year Treasury par bond, the euro against the dollar, and the 50/30/20 mixed book for both the mixed 2023 and the calm 2004-06 windows, decision D4), and a book return SHALL be the weighted sum of its components' returns between the observed days. | `tests/c11/test_c11_portfolio.py::test_portfolio_return_is_the_weighted_sum_of_components`, `tests/c11/test_c11_portfolio.py::test_portfolios_follow_decision_d4` |
| CT-604 | NO forecast SHALL use data of its own day or later: for every model of the ladder, perturbing the returns from a forecast day onward SHALL leave that day's forecast and all earlier ones bit-identical, and the estimation window of every forecast day SHALL end before the day. | `tests/c11/test_c11_cutoff.py::test_adversarial_no_forecast_uses_its_own_day_or_later` |
| CT-605 | THE hyperparameters SHALL be fixed once on 1995-01-01 to 2003-12-31, a span that ends before the earliest test window, SHALL use no observation after the span, and SHALL never be tuned on a test window. | `tests/c11/test_c11_cutoff.py::test_adversarial_hyperparameters_ignore_everything_after_the_span` |
| CT-606 | EVERY VaR, ES, fit, score, back-test, Diebold-Mariano comparison and model confidence set of C11 SHALL be computed by `riskvalidation` pinned to the commit of its `market` extra, the engine's golden values SHALL reproduce through the pin, and the C11 package SHALL define none of the engine's functions and import no estimation library directly. | `tests/c11/test_c11_engine_calls.py::test_adversarial_the_product_defines_no_engine_function`, `tests/c11/test_c11_engine_calls.py::test_every_forecast_is_an_engine_call` |
| CT-607 | THE ladder SHALL hold rungs R1 to R10 as fitted models (R10 is the quantile network trained on the FZ0 loss on each window's estimation days only, S-1 of the method tiers), THE refit frequency and the number of refits and failed fits of each rung and window SHALL be in the models document, and a fit SHALL be deterministic for a seed. | `tests/c11/test_c11_engine_calls.py::test_adversarial_the_fits_are_deterministic_for_a_seed_and_change_with_it` |
| CT-608 | THE ES of a model SHALL NOT be below its VaR on any day, a day on which the ES is undefined SHALL be listed and carry no number, and a model that forecasts no ES SHALL be marked as such and carry no FZ0. | `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_es_below_var_and_a_wrong_traffic_light_are_found` |
| CT-609 | A model SHALL be eligible only when none of the Kupiec, conditional coverage and Acerbi-Szekely rows is red at either level, and the champion at a level SHALL be the lowest average FZ0 among the eligible models with an ES on every day. | `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_a_red_gate_makes_a_model_ineligible` |
| CT-610 | WHEN a window has fewer than 250 forecast days, THE Basel traffic light SHALL be `not_evaluated` with its reason, and WHEN it has 250 or more, THE zone of each rolling 250-day window SHALL recompute from the exceedance days. | `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_es_below_var_and_a_wrong_traffic_light_are_found` |
| CT-611 | THE Acerbi-Szekely Z2 null SHALL be the engine's simulation under a tail-consistent law (an exceedance with probability alpha and an exponential excess whose mean is ES minus VaR), SHALL accept ES forecasts drawn from that law and reject an ES understated by a heavier true tail, and the simulator of the tests SHALL separate the true VaR from a misspecified one. | `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_the_z2_null_has_known_truth`, `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_the_simulator_known_truth_separates_the_oracle_from_a_misspecified_var` |
| CT-612 | EVERY C11 document SHALL validate against its schema and pass the producer checks of `tests/contracts_u8`, THE manifest SHALL list each document with its size, and a document missing from the manifest SHALL be found. | `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_a_document_missing_from_the_manifest_is_found`, `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_committed_documents_validate_pass_the_producer_checks_and_are_listed_with_their_hash` |
| CT-613 | WHEN the bake runs twice with the same seed, THE documents SHALL be byte-identical, and a different seed SHALL change the simulated nulls. | `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_adversarial_two_bakes_with_one_seed_are_identical_and_another_seed_differs` |
| CT-614 | WHERE a component of a window's portfolio is derived-only, THE realised loss series SHALL be withheld with its reason, and no committed file SHALL hold raw French returns. | `tests/c11/test_c11_readers.py::test_adversarial_no_raw_french_data_is_committed`, `tests/c11/test_c11_documents.py::test_derived_only_windows_withhold_the_realised_loss` |
| CT-615 | THE case page SHALL state the data, the derived-only rule, the Z2 null assumption, the refit frequency and the limits, and its result tables, the selection of the hyperparameters, the raw files and the hash of each document SHALL be the ones generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c11/test_c11_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_states_its_data_and_its_results_come_from_the_documents` |
