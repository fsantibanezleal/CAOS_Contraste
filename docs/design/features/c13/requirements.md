# Requirements: U8-P2, case C13 (FRTB desk eligibility: PLA and desk back-testing)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c13/`; the documents are `data/derived/C13/` (contracts `c13-models` and `c13-outputs` 1.0.0, the
producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.market` and `riskvalidation.validation.market` (engine contract `engine-market-api`
1.0.0, the consumer side). The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md).

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-1301 | THE generator SHALL state the population correlation of the hypothetical and the risk-theoretical P&L of each of the six variants in closed form from its parameters, and a large simulated sample SHALL reproduce it within the stated tolerance for every variant. | `tests/c13/test_c13_generator.py::test_a_large_sample_reproduces_the_known_correlation_of_every_variant` |
| CT-1302 | EVERY replication SHALL be exactly 250 finite days of hypothetical P&L, risk-theoretical P&L and desk VaR at 97.5 and 99 percent, and the six variants SHALL be the SDD's in order with no deficiency planted in the first and one planted in each other variant. | `tests/c13/test_c13_generator.py::test_a_replication_is_250_finite_days_and_the_six_variants_are_the_sdd_ones` |
| CT-1303 | THE replications SHALL be independent: the seed of a replication SHALL be its variant's seed base plus its index, no two replications of the bake SHALL share a seed, two replications SHALL differ, and one replication SHALL be reproduced exactly by its seed. | `tests/c13/test_c13_replications.py::test_replications_are_independent_by_seed_and_reproduced_by_their_seed` |
| CT-1304 | THE bake SHALL be deterministic: two bakes with the same seed SHALL write byte-identical documents, and two seeds SHALL write different ones. | `tests/c13/test_c13_determinism.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_write_the_same_bytes` |
| CT-1305 | EVERY PLA statistic, PLA zone, desk exception count and test row of a C13 document SHALL be returned by a `riskvalidation` function, never computed in the product, and the pinned engine SHALL reproduce the PLA and desk goldens of the engine contract. | `tests/c13/test_c13_engine_calls.py::test_the_pinned_engine_reproduces_the_contract_goldens` |
| CT-1306 | EVERY committed document of `data/derived/C13/` SHALL validate against its `c13-*` schema, pass the producer checks of `tests/contracts_u8/producer_checks.py`, and be listed by the manifest with its size and by a checksum file whose digest matches the file. | `tests/c13/test_c13_documents.py::test_every_committed_document_validates_passes_the_producer_checks_and_is_listed_with_its_hash` |
| CT-1307 | EVERY variant document SHALL name the deficiency it plants beside its parameters and the statistic it is meant to break, the no-deficiency variant SHALL report the rate of amber and red as a size and every planted variant as a power, and the variant id SHALL be the deficiency id. | `tests/c13/test_c13_documents.py::test_every_variant_carries_its_known_deficiency_and_its_role` |
| CT-1308 | THE no-deficiency variant SHALL land green at its nominal rate: its joint zone counts SHALL show at most the stated share of amber and red replications, and its desk back-tests SHALL stay within the limits at the rate of the binomial model. | `tests/c13/test_c13_replications.py::test_the_no_deficiency_variant_lands_green_at_its_nominal_rate` |
| CT-1309 | THE rate at which each planted deficiency is caught SHALL be recomputed from the replications: the zone counts SHALL close on the replications, the detection counts SHALL equal the zone counts, and the committed counts of every variant SHALL equal a fresh count of the same replications through the engine. | `tests/c13/test_c13_replications.py::test_the_reported_catch_rates_are_recomputed_from_the_replications` |
| CT-1310 | THE desk back-test SHALL count the exceptions of the hypothetical loss against the risk model's VaR at 97.5 and 99 percent through the engine, with the limits 30 and 12 of MAR32.18 to MAR32.19, and the aggregate rows SHALL be engine rows of a named replication. | `tests/c13/test_c13_documents.py::test_the_desk_backtest_rows_are_engine_rows_with_the_mar32_limits` |
| CT-1311 | THE example replication of each variant SHALL be the generator's output for the seed it names, and its Spearman, KS and zone SHALL recompute from its stored series through the engine. | `tests/c13/test_c13_documents.py::test_the_example_is_the_generator_output_for_its_seed_and_recomputes` |
| CT-1312 | NO text of C13 SHALL present the generated desk P&L as observations of a real desk: every document and the case page SHALL carry the synthetic notice. | `tests/c13/test_c13_documents.py::test_no_text_presents_the_generated_pnl_as_a_real_desk` |
| CT-1313 | THE results block of `docs/cases/C13.md` SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c13/test_c13_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_results_block_is_generated_from_the_committed_documents` |
