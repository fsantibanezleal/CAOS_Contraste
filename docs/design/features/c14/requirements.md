# Requirements: U10-P1, case C14 (IRRBB six shocks on a USD balance sheet, SVB 2022)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c14/`; the documents are `data/derived/C14/` (contracts `c14-outputs` and `c14-models` 1.0.0, the
producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.irrbb` and `riskvalidation.validation.irrbb` (engine contract `engine-irrbb-api`
1.1.0, the consumer side). The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md).

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-1401 | THE FDIC reader SHALL return only the call reports dated on or before a valuation date, SHALL refuse a report that does not exist at that date, and SHALL read a later report only through the out-of-sample function, which SHALL refuse a period that does not start after the date. | `tests/c14/test_c14_readers.py::test_reports_up_to_a_date_never_include_a_later_one` |
| CT-1402 | THE Treasury reader SHALL return the last par curve published on or before a date (a weekend carries the previous business day), SHALL list a tenor the day lacks and SHALL NOT fill it. | `tests/c14/test_c14_readers.py::test_treasury_curve_as_of_never_reads_a_later_day_and_lists_missing_tenors`, `tests/test_io_treasury.py::test_history_up_to_holds_no_curve_after_the_date` |
| CT-1403 | THE raw FDIC and Treasury files SHALL each have a provenance record with URL, size, SHA256 and retrieval date that matches the file bytes, and the committed `data/derived/C14/raw-sources.txt` SHALL repeat the records in the format of `data/sources/manifest.json`. | `tests/c14/test_c14_readers.py::test_the_raw_files_match_their_provenance_records_and_the_committed_copy` |
| CT-1404 | EVERY balance-sheet item of a document SHALL slot its reported call-report amount into the 19 buckets with a named slotting assumption (the bands of the report spread over the buckets they overlap), the items SHALL sum to the reported total assets and total liabilities, and an amount the report does not carry SHALL be a plug with its reason and no reported amount. | `tests/c14/test_c14_slotting.py::test_every_item_slots_its_reported_amount_and_the_sheet_reconciles_to_the_report` |
| CT-1405 | EVERY behavioural assumption (NMD core share and maturity, prepayment rate, term-deposit redemption rate, deposit beta, the open band edges) SHALL be a named field with its value, unit, source kind and source, and an assumption that is not in the call report SHALL be stated synthetic. | `tests/c14/test_c14_slotting.py::test_every_behavioural_assumption_is_named_with_its_source_and_stated_synthetic` |
| CT-1406 | AN NMD core share or maturity requested above its SRP31 Table 4 cap SHALL be cut to the cap and SHALL carry `cap_applied`, and the NMD items SHALL slot the used values. | `tests/c14/test_c14_documents.py::test_nmd_requests_above_the_cap_are_cut_and_flagged_and_slotted_at_the_used_values` |
| CT-1407 | EVERY EVE, delta EVE, delta NII, outlier test, simulated NII, EaR, EVaR and Monte Carlo error of a C14 document SHALL equal a `riskvalidation` call on the inputs the document carries, and the pinned engine SHALL reproduce the contract goldens. | `tests/c14/test_c14_engine_calls.py::test_every_number_of_a_document_equals_the_engine_call_on_its_inputs` |
| CT-1408 | EVERY committed C14 document SHALL validate against its `c14-*` schema, pass `check_document`, say that its aggregates are real and its behaviour synthetic, and be listed by the manifest with its size and by a checksum file whose digest matches the file. | `tests/c14/test_c14_documents.py::test_every_committed_document_validates_passes_the_semantic_checks_and_is_listed_with_its_hash` |
| CT-1409 | EVERY valuation date SHALL carry the EVE risk measure from exactly six shock rows under the 2024 and the 2016 calibrations, each with and without behavioural NMD, and the comparison rows SHALL equal the scenarios. | `tests/c14/test_c14_documents.py::test_every_valuation_date_carries_six_shocks_in_four_scenarios_with_their_twins` |
| CT-1410 | THE EaR and EVaR of a valuation date SHALL carry the Monte Carlo errors of the engine for each level, the method, the number of paths, the seed and the batches, and two bakes with the same seed SHALL write the same bytes. | `tests/c14/test_c14_documents.py::test_ear_evar_levels_carry_the_engine_monte_carlo_errors` |
| CT-1411 | THE out-of-sample row of 2021-Q4 SHALL compare the projected parallel-up NII change with the NII realised in 2022 from the call report of 2022-12-31, and the later valuation dates SHALL state that no realised period is available. | `tests/c14/test_c14_documents.py::test_the_out_of_sample_row_compares_2021_q4_with_the_realised_2022_nii` |
| CT-1412 | NO value of a document SHALL be dated after its valuation date: the call reports, the curve, the balance sheet and the fitted models SHALL use only data up to the date, and removing every later report from the inputs SHALL not change a variant but its out-of-sample row. | `tests/c14/test_c14_documents.py::test_no_value_is_dated_after_its_valuation_date_and_later_data_changes_nothing` |
| CT-1413 | THE bake SHALL be deterministic: two bakes with the same seed SHALL write byte-identical documents. | `tests/c14/test_c14_determinism.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_write_the_same_bytes` |
| CT-1414 | THE results block of `docs/cases/C14.md` SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c14/test_c14_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_results_block_is_generated_from_the_committed_documents` |
