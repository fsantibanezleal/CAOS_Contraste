# Requirements: U10-P2, case C15 (IRRBB on a CLP and UF balance sheet, RAN 21-13 Annex 1)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c15/`, the reader is `data-pipeline/pipeline/io/bcch.py`; the documents are `data/derived/C15/` (contracts
`c15-outputs` and `c15-models` 1.0.0, the producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.irrbb` and `riskvalidation.validation.irrbb`
(engine contract `engine-irrbb-api` 1.1.0, the consumer side). The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are
[`validation.md`](validation.md).
