# Requirements: U11-P, case C16 (liquidity stress, LCR and the March 2023 run, on the call reports of the bank that failed on 2023-03-10)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c16/`; the documents are `data/derived/C16/` (contracts `c16-outputs`, `c16-outputs-reverse` and
`c16-models`, the producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.liquidity`, `riskvalidation.regulatory.lcr_nsfr` and
`riskvalidation.validation.liquidity` (engine contract `engine-liquidity-api` 1.2.0, the consumer side). The design is
[`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md).
