# Requirements: U12-P, case C17 (operational risk: LDA against SMA)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c17/`; the documents are `data/derived/C17/` (contracts `c17-models` and `c17-outputs` 1.0.0, the
producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.oprisk` and `riskvalidation.validation.oprisk` (engine contract
`engine-oprisk-api` 1.0.0, the consumer side, pinned by commit until the engine release is tagged). The design is
[`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md).

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-1701 | THE generator scale of each variant SHALL be the least squares fit, on the log excess quantiles, of a GPD with the variant's tail index to the four LDCE median percentiles, and the fitted quantiles SHALL stay within the stated tolerance of the targets, which SHALL carry their source and the derived-only label. | `tests/c17/test_c17_calibration.py::test_the_fitted_quantiles_reproduce_the_ldce_percentiles_within_the_tolerance` |
| CT-1702 | THE generator frequency SHALL give the LDCE median of 0.82 losses of EUR 20,000 or more per EUR billion of assets, so that the expected count of losses of at least EUR 20,000 of the synthetic bank equals 0.82 times its assets in billions. | `tests/c17/test_c17_calibration.py::test_the_generator_counts_the_ldce_median_frequency_above_20000` |
| CT-1703 | THE true 99.9 percent annual loss quantile of each variant SHALL be computed once by the engine on a fine grid, SHALL agree between the Fourier and the Panjer aggregation within 0.1 percent, and SHALL agree with a Monte Carlo of the generator within three standard errors. | `tests/c17/test_c17_truth.py::test_the_true_quantile_agrees_across_fft_panjer_and_monte_carlo` |
| CT-1704 | THE true 99.9 percent quantile SHALL increase strictly with the tail index across the four variants, and no mean SHALL be stated for a tail index of 1 or more. | `tests/c17/test_c17_truth.py::test_the_true_quantile_increases_with_the_tail_index_and_no_mean_at_one_or_more` |
| CT-1705 | THE bank SHALL record only the losses at or above the collection threshold, and every fit, rate and annual total of a replication SHALL read the recorded losses only; the truth SHALL count every loss the generator draws at or above its floor, whatever the threshold. | `tests/c17/test_c17_generator.py::test_losses_below_the_collection_threshold_are_removed_before_every_fit` |
| CT-1706 | THE rows of every replication SHALL pass the loss_events family of contract 1 with the collection threshold of its cell and the currency EUR, with no rejected row. | `tests/c17/test_c17_generator.py::test_every_recorded_history_passes_the_loss_events_contract` |
| CT-1707 | THE replications SHALL be independent and reproducible: one history is reproduced exactly by its seed, two seeds give different histories, and the seed ranges of the cells of a variant are disjoint. | `tests/c17/test_c17_generator.py::test_a_history_is_reproduced_by_its_seed_and_the_seed_ranges_are_disjoint` |
| CT-1708 | THE pinned engine SHALL reproduce the goldens of the engine contract that the bake relies on (the Panjer benchmark quantile 5849, the BIC of EUR 5.37 billion at BI EUR 35 billion, the single loss approximation 6124.555320, the ILM of 1 when the loss component equals the BIC), and the pin SHALL name an engine commit. | `tests/c17/test_c17_engine_calls.py::test_the_pinned_engine_reproduces_the_contract_goldens` |
| CT-1709 | EVERY capital, quantile, fit, ILM and test of a C17 document SHALL be returned by a `riskvalidation` function: the package SHALL contain no formula of the LDA, of the single loss approximation or of the standardised approach, and a capital SHALL change when the engine function that returns it changes. | `tests/c17/test_c17_engine_calls.py::test_every_capital_comes_from_the_engine_and_the_product_holds_no_formula` |
| CT-1710 | EVERY committed document of `data/derived/C17/` SHALL validate against its `c17-*` schema, pass `semantic_findings`, and be listed by the manifest with its size and a checksum file whose digest matches. | `tests/c17/test_c17_documents.py::test_every_committed_document_validates_passes_the_semantic_gate_and_is_listed_with_its_hash` |
| CT-1711 | A variant with a tail index of 1 or more SHALL state no true mean, no mean-corrected single loss approximation and no expected shortfall, with the reason, and every variant document SHALL carry its truth block with the generator's true parameters and true quantile. | `tests/c17/test_c17_documents.py::test_a_tail_index_of_one_or_more_states_no_mean_and_every_document_carries_its_truth` |
| CT-1712 | AN ILM SHALL never be computed on fewer than 5 years of loss data: every cell has 5 or 10 years, the standardised approach SHALL be called with the 5-year minimum, and a history of fewer than 5 years SHALL give the BIC only with its reason. | `tests/c17/test_c17_documents.py::test_an_ilm_is_never_computed_on_fewer_than_five_years` |
| CT-1713 | EVERY document and the case page SHALL say that the data are synthetic, SHALL present the bank as synthetic, and SHALL NOT present the LDCE statistics as the losses of any bank. | `tests/c17/test_c17_documents.py::test_no_text_presents_the_synthetic_bank_as_real` |
| CT-1714 | THE bake SHALL be deterministic: two bakes with the same seed SHALL write byte-identical documents, and a different seed SHALL write different ones. | `tests/c17/test_c17_determinism.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_write_the_same_bytes` |
| CT-1715 | THE results block of `docs/cases/C17.md` SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c17/test_c17_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_results_block_is_generated_from_the_committed_documents` |
