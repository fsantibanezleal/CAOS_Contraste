# Requirements: U13-P1, case C18 (ICAAP and IAPE capital aggregation under macro scenarios)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c18/`; the documents are `data/derived/C18/` (contracts `c18-outputs`, `c18-outputs-aggregation`,
`c18-outputs-reverse` and `c18-models` 1.0.0, the producer side, with the cross-field checks of `tests/contracts_u13/semantics.py`); the
methods are `riskvalidation.aggregation`, `riskvalidation.stress` and their validation tests (engine contracts `engine-aggregation-api` 1.1.0
and `engine-stress-api`, the consumer side). The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md).

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-1801 | THE Federal Reserve reader SHALL read the history and the two 2026 supervisory scenarios by column name from the agency columns only (never the equity, house price, commercial real estate price, volatility or BBB columns), SHALL require the history to end at the jump-off 2025 Q4 and each scenario to hold 13 quarters, and a missing Federal Reserve file SHALL stop C18 with the URL it comes from. | `tests/c18/test_c18_readers.py::test_fed_inputs_are_the_agency_columns_and_a_missing_file_names_its_url` |
| CT-1802 | THE net charge-off rates SHALL be read signed as the Board prints them, the commercial and residential real estate series SHALL start in 1991 Q1, and no quarter SHALL be floored, filled or dropped. | `tests/c18/test_c18_readers.py::test_chargeoff_rates_are_signed_and_the_real_estate_series_start_in_1991` |
| CT-1803 | THE EBA reader SHALL read the United States rows of the macro-financial workbook by title and country code, SHALL refuse a layout it does not know, and a missing workbook SHALL leave the EBA variant unwritten with the URL it comes from. | `tests/c18/test_c18_readers.py::test_eba_reader_reads_the_us_rows_and_refuses_an_unknown_layout` |
| CT-1804 | THE NGFS reader SHALL read the Baseline levels and the Delayed transition deviations of the four NiGEM variables of the United States, a scenario level SHALL be the baseline plus the deviation (the percent difference for GDP), and the policy-shock window SHALL be 2031 to 2033. | `tests/c18/test_c18_readers.py::test_ngfs_levels_are_the_baseline_plus_the_deviation_over_the_shock_window` |
| CT-1805 | THE EUR to CLP rate SHALL be the last observation of the BCCh series on or before the as-of date, SHALL record the series id it read, and the operational asset scaling SHALL be Banco Andino's assets over the C17 bank's assets converted at that rate. | `tests/c18/test_c18_readers.py::test_the_eur_clp_rate_records_its_series_and_the_asset_scaling_uses_it` |
| CT-1806 | EVERY satellite SHALL be fitted by `riskvalidation.stress.satellites.fit_satellite` on a window that ends in 2019 Q4 (1991 Q1 to 2019 Q4 with the identity link for the two real estate segments, 1985 Q1 to 2019 Q4 with the probit link for the other three), the models document SHALL record each segment's link and window start, and a fit that reaches a quarter of 2020 SHALL be refused. | `tests/c18/test_c18_satellites.py::test_satellite_windows_end_in_2019_and_the_links_follow_the_signs` |
| CT-1807 | THE back-cast SHALL run the fitted satellites from 2019 Q4 on the realised regressors of 2020 Q1 to 2025 Q4 and compare them with the realised rates, 2026 Q1 and Q2 SHALL be rate-only descriptive rows with no forecast claim, and every back-cast and sign test row SHALL be an engine test result. | `tests/c18/test_c18_satellites.py::test_backcast_is_out_of_time_and_2026_is_descriptive` |
| CT-1808 | EVERY scenario path SHALL be the published path transposed onto the last observed value by the engine's path functions, SHALL carry its vintage, and no vintage SHALL postdate the observation date. | `tests/c18/test_c18_scenarios.py::test_paths_are_transposed_by_the_engine_and_no_vintage_postdates_the_as_of_date` |
| CT-1809 | EVERY marginal SHALL be at a horizon of one year and a confidence level of 99.9 percent, its EC SHALL be its VaR less its expected loss, and its VaR, ES and expected loss SHALL be those of the engine's estimators on the sample or distribution the aggregation uses. | `tests/c18/test_c18_marginals.py::test_every_marginal_is_one_year_999_and_ec_is_var_less_el` |
| CT-1810 | THE market marginal SHALL be C11's mixed-portfolio VaR and ES moved to 99.9 percent by the normal quantile ratio and to one year by the square root of 250, both recorded as conventions, and the operational marginal SHALL be `oprisk.aggregate` on the C17 `xi-0-8` parameters scaled by assets and converted at the BCCh rate, an infinite-mean severity being refused. | `tests/c18/test_c18_marginals.py::test_market_scaling_conventions_and_the_operational_marginal` |
| CT-1811 | THE headline capital of every scenario document SHALL be the simple sum of the four marginal EC, and every other row of the band, the Euler allocation, the diversification and the model risk measures SHALL be labelled information and not capital. | `tests/c18/test_c18_aggregation.py::test_the_headline_is_the_simple_sum_and_every_other_figure_is_information` |
| CT-1812 | THE band SHALL hold the lower bound, the simple sum, variance-covariance, the Gaussian copula, one t copula row for each nu of the named set and the worst case over the same marginals, the correlation matrices SHALL be the named conventions (the Rosenberg and Schuermann base and market-credit 0.8 as the stress), and the Euler contributions SHALL add up to the aggregate expected shortfall. | `tests/c18/test_c18_aggregation.py::test_the_band_has_the_ladder_rows_and_the_euler_allocation_adds_up` |
| CT-1813 | THE headline CET1 path SHALL be the loss-only depletion, the PPNR SHALL be a labelled row with the named fee and cost ratios, the OI SHALL be the sum of its components with the headroom, and the 4 percent cap SHALL bind the art. 66 quinquies requirement `p2r` and never the bank's own `c_p2`. | `tests/c18/test_c18_capital.py::test_loss_only_headline_ppnr_row_oi_and_the_cap_on_p2r` |

| CT-1815 | THE reverse stress SHALL take the Chilean legal minimum stack as the failure point and the standardised satellite regressors with their 1985 to 2019 covariance as the factors, SHALL start from at least three points, and its solution SHALL satisfy the engine's solution check. | `tests/c18/test_c18_reverse.py::test_reverse_stress_reaches_the_stack_with_the_minimum_distance` |
| CT-1816 | EVERY committed C18 document SHALL validate against its `c18-*` schema, return no finding from `semantics.violations`, return none from `semantics.cross_violations` across the set, and be listed by the manifest and by a checksum file whose digests match the bytes. | `tests/c18/test_c18_documents.py::test_every_committed_document_validates_and_the_set_agrees` |
| CT-1817 | AN absent NGFS read or EBA workbook SHALL leave that variant unwritten and the other variants unaffected, each skipped variant naming its URL; an absent Federal Reserve table, charge-off page or EUR to CLP series, or a missing committed document of C07, C15 or C17, SHALL stop C18. | `tests/c18/test_c18_missing_data.py::test_a_missing_optional_source_skips_its_variant_and_a_missing_required_one_stops_the_case` |

| CT-1819 | THE bake SHALL be deterministic: two bakes with the same seed SHALL write byte-identical documents. | `tests/c18/test_c18_determinism.py::test_two_bakes_with_the_same_seed_write_the_same_bytes` |
| CT-1820 | THE C18 package SHALL compute no value the engine provides: every VaR, ES, quantile, fit, projection, aggregate, band row, capital row and test result SHALL come from a `riskvalidation` call. | `tests/c18/test_c18_engine_calls.py::test_no_statistic_of_the_engine_is_computed_in_the_package` |
| CT-1821 | THE results block of `docs/cases/C18.md` SHALL be the one generated from the committed documents. | `tests/c18/test_c18_case_page.py::test_the_case_page_results_block_is_generated_from_the_committed_documents` |

| CT-1823 | THE NII of the PPNR row SHALL follow each variant's rate path (the short rate where the variant carries one, otherwise its 10-year rate, named per variant) through the engine's `delta_nii` on C15's book and curve with C15's floors, the IRRBB loss of a scenario SHALL be nil because an EVE change is not a profit-and-loss item, and the documents SHALL state that the market and operational losses are expected losses and the stressed loss a floor. | `tests/c18/test_c18_nii.py::test_nii_follows_the_variant_rate_path_and_the_floor_is_stated` |
| CT-1824 | THE capital start SHALL label the T2 carrying of the PE above CET1 an UNVERIFIED assumption, and the headline statements SHALL say that the stressed credit path rests on satellites that fail their own back-cast. | `tests/c18/test_c18_documents_text.py::test_the_labels_of_the_capital_start_and_the_headline_are_stated` |
