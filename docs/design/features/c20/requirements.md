# Requirements: U14-P2, case C20 (ML model validation: drift, robustness, explanation stability)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c20/`; the documents are `data/derived/C20/` (contracts `c20-outputs`, `c20-outputs-shift`,
`c20-outputs-seeds`, `c20-outputs-perturbation`, `c20-outputs-leakage` and `c20-models` 1.0.0, the producer side); the methods are
`riskvalidation.ml`, `riskvalidation.fairness` and `riskvalidation.validation` (engine contracts `engine-ml-api` and `engine-fairness-api`,
the consumer side, read at the pinned engine commit, where the engine is binding over the 1.0.0 page of `engine-ml-api`). The design is
[`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md). Nothing here certifies a model or claims compliance
(SDD non-goal 1); the SGCD data must not be used for real-world credit scoring.
