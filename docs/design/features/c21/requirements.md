# Requirements: U14-P3, case C21 (fair-lending disparity testing on HMDA decisions, Delaware, 2018 to 2024)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The bake is
`data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c21/`; the documents are `data/derived/C21/` (contracts `c21-outputs` and `c21-models` 1.0.0, the
producer side); the methods are `riskvalidation.fairness` and `riskvalidation.validation.fairness` (engine contract
`engine-fairness-api` 1.2.0, the consumer side). The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are
[`validation.md`](validation.md). The case measures statistical disparity in public decisions about people and scores nobody:
every document says "no legal claim" (SDD non-goals 1 and 3).
