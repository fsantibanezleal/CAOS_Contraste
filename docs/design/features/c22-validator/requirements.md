# Requirements: U3, case C22 (validating the validator: the size and power of every test)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-301 | THE C22 null variant SHALL report, for every registered riskvalidation test with a p-value, the rejection rate at 5% and at 1% on a generator where the test's null holds at its boundary with its assumptions met, from at least 4,000 repetitions, each rate with its Monte Carlo standard error and Wilson interval, the p-value histogram, and whether it lies within the size bound. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_size_of_every_test_reported` |
| CT-302 | FOR each defect family (miscalibration, clustering, drift, discrimination decay, leakage, broken monotonicity, concentration), THE C22 variant SHALL report the rejection rate of every test it targets at severities 0 to 5, at 5% and 1%, each with its Monte Carlo standard error, with the severity ladder and its unit stated, and with the generator's configuration and truth committed in the artifact. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_power_curves_complete` |
| CT-303 | WHERE a rejection probability is exact (the count tests; the traffic lights with independent years), THE artifact SHALL carry the exact value beside the simulated one, and every simulated rate SHALL agree with it within 3.29 Monte Carlo standard errors. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_exact_agrees_with_simulation` |
| CT-304 | THE clustering variant SHALL carry BCBS WP14 Tables 7 and 8 and the drift variant Yurdakul and Naranjo Table 4, cell by cell with the published value, the measured one and the z of the difference (143 of 144 cells agreeing in WP14; at least 48 of 54 in Yurdakul and Naranjo, where the harness sits slightly above the paper on average and how many cells pass depends on the seed, the offset stated with its mean z), and the leakage variant Demler et al.'s nested-discriminant result, the cells that do not agree named. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_published_reproductions_carried` |
| CT-305 | A C22 finding SHALL be evidenced by measured rates in its artifact (`rate:` evidence naming a simulation key of the outputs), and the contract SHALL refuse a finding whose `rate:` evidence names no simulation of the artifact. | `tests/test_licence_lineage.py::test_rate_evidence_names_a_simulation`, `tests/test_c22.py::test_findings_cite_rates` |
| CT-306 | EVERY C22 variant SHALL carry truth status `synthetic-known-truth` and name its generators in the lineage; the bake SHALL be a pure function of the seed, two bakes giving identical artifacts. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_lineage_and_determinism` |
| CT-307 | WHEN the bake runs C22, THE pipeline SHALL fail if a result lies outside the range the case declares, naming the result. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_expected_ranges_hold` |
| CT-308 | THE web SHALL compute live the exact size and power of the binomial, Vasicek-corrected binomial and Jeffreys tests for a reader's obligors, PD applied, ratio of true to applied PD, true and assumed asset correlations and level, and the TypeScript results SHALL equal riskvalidation's on the exported parity points within 1e-9 relative. | `frontend/src/engine/sizepower.test.ts` |
| CT-309 | THE C22 instrument SHALL keep the six groups in order, show the lane and the provenance on every view, and hold a live read-out in each rail section that changes with its controls. | `frontend/src/workbench/c22.test.tsx` |
| CT-310 | WHEN the measured gate walks C22 at five sizes, both themes and both languages, THE site SHALL pass every check. | `frontend/scripts/gate.mjs` (G1 to G14) |
| CT-311 | THE C05 generator SHALL be riskvalidation's `DefaultCounts`, and the C05 bake SHALL stay identical on every number. | `tests/test_c05.py::test_generator_is_the_engines` |
| CT-312 | THE C01 Hosmer-Lemeshow test on the holdout SHALL use as many degrees of freedom as groups (PDs fitted on other data), as riskvalidation 0.3.0 does by default. | `tests/test_c01.py::test_hosmer_lemeshow_uses_g_on_the_holdout` |
| CT-313 | THE C01 test of the AUC against the initial AUC SHALL add the variance of the calibration slice's AUC to its statistic, the slice being disjoint from every evaluation set, and say so in the result. | `tests/test_c01.py::test_auc_vs_initial_carries_the_development_variance` |
