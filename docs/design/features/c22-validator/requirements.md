# Requirements: U3, case C22 (validating the validator: the size and power of every test)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-301 | THE C22 null variant SHALL report, for every p-value test the installed riskvalidation registers, in every family (the five credit families, discrimination, PD calibration, rating system, stability and transitions, and the other seven, counterparty credit risk, IFRS 9, LGD and EAD, market, portfolio, fairness and machine learning; no family filter, 42 tests), the rejection rate at 5% and at 1% on a generator where the test's null holds at its boundary with its assumptions met (for the 21 tests outside the credit families, the null of the engine's named study in `riskvalidation.generators.size_studies.STUDIES`, run through `simulate_study` with no simulation written in the product), from at least 4,000 repetitions, each rate with its Monte Carlo standard error and Wilson interval, the p-value histogram, and whether it lies within the size bound; a registered test without a study, a missing optional extra of the engine and a repetition without a p-value SHALL stop the bake, naming it. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_size_of_every_test_reported`, `tests/test_c22_all_families.py::test_the_catalogue_and_the_null_variant_agree_without_a_family_filter`, `tests/test_c22_studies.py::test_a_registered_test_without_a_study_stops_the_bake_naming_it` |
| CT-302 | FOR each defect family (miscalibration, clustering, drift, discrimination decay, leakage, broken monotonicity, concentration), THE C22 variant SHALL report the rejection rate of every test it targets at severities 0 to 5, at 5% and 1%, each with its Monte Carlo standard error, with the severity ladder and its unit stated, and with the generator's configuration and truth committed in the artifact. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_power_curves_complete` |
| CT-303 | WHERE a rejection probability is exact (the count tests; the traffic lights with independent years), THE artifact SHALL carry the exact value beside the simulated one, and every simulated rate SHALL agree with it within 3.29 Monte Carlo standard errors. | `tests/test_c22.py::test_exact_agrees_with_simulation` |
