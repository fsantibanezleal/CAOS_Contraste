# Requirements: SHOW-1, the guided story and the visualization catalog (ADR-0082)

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The story is
`/story/<step>` (`frontend/src/pages/Story.tsx`, `frontend/src/story/`) on the shell's `StoryFrame`; it reads the committed
governance documents (`data/derived/governance/`) and the C11 window documents (`data/derived/C11/`) through the product's loaders
and adds no artifact. The design is [`design.md`](design.md); the adversarial checks are [`validation.md`](validation.md). The
catalog of the six view kinds is the SDD section `## Visualization catalog`.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| CT-960 | THE story SHALL have five steps (inventory, backtest, crisis, recalibrate, decide) at `/story/<step>`, declared in `product.json` as `showcase.story_routes` (the gate runner and the static build read that declaration), each directly linked, written by the static build as a deep link, drawing its own view only, with an exit that lands on the route the reader came from (`from`). | `frontend/scripts/check_story_contraste.mjs` (and `frontend/scripts/check_story_mode.mjs` for what every story owes) |
| CT-961 | THE headline of each step SHALL state a finding with its number read from the committed artifacts, in at most 12 words, in both languages, for every window and every model. | `frontend/src/story/story.test.tsx::every headline of every window and every model, in both languages, has its number in at most 12 words` |
| CT-962 | THE flow of the bank SHALL count its findings from the register: the findings of the flow SHALL equal the register in total and by severity, and each family SHALL hold the findings its cases hold in `counts.by_case`. | `frontend/src/story/model.test.ts::the findings of the flow are the findings of the document, in total and by severity` |
| CT-963 | THE flow SHALL place every inventory entry by its case and lose none; an entry of a case it does not place SHALL be reported, never put in a family. | `frontend/src/story/model.test.ts::places every inventory entry and loses none` |
