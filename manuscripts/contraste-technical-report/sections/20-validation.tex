\section{Validation}
\label{sec:validation}

A table of results is only as good as the evidence that the numbers are right. This section collects that evidence: what each oracle of the protocol (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}) established, with the figures from the method sections, and the tests that guard each result
so that a change which breaks one is caught before a reader sees it. The measured error rates of the validation tests, which tell a reader how far to trust a light, are the first and the most important part.

\paragraph{The validation tests are measured, not assumed.} The validation of the validator (case C22) simulates every registered test of the engine under its null and under defects of known severity. It runs \pxOneSims{} simulations and \pxOneReps{} seeded repetitions in \pxOneFamilies{} families, on \pxOneGens{} generators whose truth reproduces from the committed configuration, and it reports \pxOneRates{} rates, each with its Monte Carlo standard error and its Wilson interval, with an exact rejection probability beside \pxOneExact{} of them (\cref{sec:bs1}). Of the \pxOneTests{} tests that decide by a p-value, each simulated under its null in \rsCTwentyTwoNullPerTest{} repetitions (\rsCTwentyTwoNullReps{} in all), \rsCTwentyTwoWithin{} hold their size at 5 and 1 percent. The two that do not show what the measurement is for: the Jeffreys test of a portfolio, whose exact rejection probability at a nominal 1 percent is \rsCTwentyTwoJeffExactOne{}, and the normal test over five years, which rejects \rsCTwentyTwoNormal{} of null samples at a nominal 5 percent against a Monte Carlo bound of \rsCTwentyTwoBound{} and \rsCTwentyTwoNormalOne{} at a nominal 1 percent against a bound of \rsCTwentyTwoBoundOne{}. Two settings outside that count show the same thing. The Hosmer--Lemeshow test with $G-2$ degrees of freedom on a validation sample, where the standard result holds only for probabilities fitted on the same sample, rejects \rsCTwentyTwoHl{} of null samples at a nominal 5 percent against the same bound of \rsCTwentyTwoBound{}, and the population stability index above 0.10 raises a false alarm in \rsCTwentyTwoPsi{} of samples of 100 in which nothing changed, so the common threshold is a convention with a high false alarm rate in small samples and not a statistical limit. The published studies of the size and power of these tests are rerun by exact enumeration and simulation, and their cells agree or the disagreement is stated (\cref{sec:m37}).

\paragraph{Golden values reproduce, with the gaps located.} The printed numbers of five papers in the rating and calibration cases are recomputed beside their print: \pxTwoReproduced{} of \pxTwoPrinted{} are reproduced to the printed digit, and every other one is listed with its cell and its explanation (\cref{sec:bs2}). Of the \rtgPtCells{} cells of the low-default tables of Pluto and Tasche, \rtgPtIdent{} are identical to the printed digits and the largest gap is \rtgPtMaxgap{} percentage points; of the forecast cells of Tasche, \rtgGoldSevenIdent{} of \rtgGoldSevenN{} are identical and all lie within the tolerance of the engine; and \rtgIrwAgree{} of the \rtgIrwTotal{} generator distances of Israel, Rosenthal and Wei agree. The Basel interest-rate risk standard is reproduced for the short-rate-up shock at \almSrpShortUp{} basis points, the steepener at \almSrpSteepener{} and the flattener at \almSrpFlattener{}. The operational-risk benchmark has a mean of \opBenchMean{} and a 99.9 percent quantile of \opBenchPanjer{} by Panjer recursion and \opBenchFft{} by fast Fourier transform, against \opBenchMcQ{} with a Monte Carlo standard error of \opBenchMcSe{}.

\paragraph{Known truth bounds the estimators.} Where a generator fixes the truth, the estimators are measured against it: the conversion factor of C09 is recovered with a bias that the design determines (Section~\ref{sec:results}), the cohort estimator of the rating section gives a probability of exactly zero for the best grades in a share of panels that is reported, and the dispersion of the operational-risk capital is measured over \pxTwelveHistories{} independent histories (\cref{sec:bs12}). The generators are design choices of the product, so a rate measured on them is not the probability of detecting a real bank's problem; each claim is stated with the generator and the severity ladder it was measured on.

\paragraph{Identities and invariants hold.} The row sums of the transition matrices of the three agencies deviate from one by at most $2\times10^{-16}$; the Euler contributions of the aggregation add up to the total within \agstEulerErr{}, against a tolerance of \agstEulerTol{}; and \ccrInvariantHold{} of \ccrInvariantRows{} counterparty invariants hold (\cref{sec:ccr}). The closed-form Hull--White quantities agree with QuantLib to a relative $\ccrQlMaxRel{}$ in \ccrQlRows{} rows.

\paragraph{Independent reconciliation.} The p-values of the Jeffreys and binomial tests are recomputed by a quadrature of the Beta density and by an exact rational sum of the binomial terms, neither of which calls the functions of the engine, and agree within $10^{-9}$ on a grid of \pxTwoGridCases{} cases. The exposure of the single swap and of the four-swap netting set agrees with the Open Source Risk Engine \pxTwoOreVersion{} within two combined Monte Carlo standard errors at all \pxTwoSingleDates{} report dates (the largest gap is \pxTwoSingleMax{} for the single swap and \pxTwoNettingMax{} for the netting set), and so do the effective expected positive exposure and the credit valuation adjustment. The collateralised variants do not reconcile: the largest exposure gaps are \pxTwoCsaTenMax{}, \pxTwoCsaTwentyMax{} and \pxTwoImMax{} combined standard errors for the 10-day, 20-day and initial-margin variants, and the credit valuation adjustments differ by \pxTwoCsaTenCva{} and \pxTwoCsaTwentyCva{} standard errors in the two collateral variants. These gaps are reported and not claimed as agreement.

\paragraph{The tests that guard each result.} Each requirement of the design names the test that guards it. The repository collects \rsPytest{} Python tests for the pipeline, the contracts, the guards, the artifacts and the cases, \rsAdversarial{} negative-control plants that insert a defect and require the guard to fail, and \rsVitest{} tests of the web. The plants matter because a guard that never fails proves nothing: a leakage plant, for example, must be caught by the leakage test (\cref{sec:bs3}). The committed artifacts are checked against their schemas in both directions, and the contract digests are locked. The findings register is checked by digest: its SHA-256 \rsShaFindingsMatch{} the value listed in the checksum file, and \rsShaMatching{} of the \rsShaEntries{} artifacts of the governance folder match their digests. The numbers of this report are regenerated by the scripts of Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}, so a change in an artifact changes the text.

\paragraph{The units gate.} A method must answer the question it was asked, so each quantity is checked to be in the units of its question before a result is shown. In Contraste the gate has three parts. First, every headline quantity of a case document is stored as a value with its unit and a bilingual label, the schema requires the three, and the bake refuses a document whose quantity lacks one or is not a finite number. Second, the schemas bound the quantities whose range is fixed by definition: a probability of default is open at 0 and 1 at every estimate site, and a loss given default is closed at 0 and 1 where a site carries one. The one place where a probability of default of exactly 0 is accepted is the parity grid of the capital function, where 0 probes the regulatory floor and is not an estimate; a test refuses a probability of exactly 0 or 1 at an estimate site and accepts 0 at that grid. Third, a conversion factor and a value at risk or expected shortfall are stated with the convention or the portfolio unit of their case, in the unit field of the quantity. The gate covers the documents that are committed, which the schemas check in both directions; it does not recompute a method on a rescaled input, so a method that returns a number in the wrong scale inside the allowed range would pass it.

\paragraph{What this validation does not establish.} A reproduced golden value shows that the engine computes what the paper computed, not that the paper's method is right for a given bank. A reconciliation with a reference engine shows agreement with that engine's model, and the wrong-way variant is not reconciled because the example of the reference engine is a different model. The leakage tests see a leak as a drop of discrimination out of time, not as a drift of the leaked feature. The lights of the validation reports inherit the size of their tests, which the first paragraph of this section measures.
