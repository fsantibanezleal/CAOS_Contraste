\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Ranking holds where calibration breaks.} The recurrent pattern of the credit cases is that discrimination survives what calibration does not. The scorecard of C03 keeps an AUC of \rsCThreeAuc{} on the cohort that lived through 2008--2010 while the observed five-year probability of default is \rsCThreeObs{}\,\% against \rsCThreePred{}\,\% predicted, and in C08 the calibration by segment ends red in \LE{cal-red} of \LE{cal-total} cohort-by-rung tests while every discrimination test is green. A model trained on benign years orders borrowers by the same risk factors in a crisis, and it sizes the loss by the level of the years it saw. For a provision or a capital charge the level is what is paid for, so a validator who reads only the AUC passes a model that a calibration test in the same battery fails. The supervisors' complaint that thresholds are missing for discriminatory power and stability, and that back-testing is incomplete \citep{fr:ecbtrim}, points at the same place: the evidence a validation file needs is the calibration out of time, with a stated threshold.

\paragraph{The design of the estimate moves the answer as much as the model.} The spread of the IRB risk weight of C05 over \rsCFiveApproaches{} calibration approaches (\rsCFiveRwMin{}\,\% to \rsCFiveRwMax{}\,\% of the exposure), the untreated bias of the conversion factor of C09 under three observation designs (\rsCNineFixed{}, \rsCNineCohort{} and \rsCNineVariable{} against a truth of \rsCNineTruth{}) and the gap between the three provisioning regimes of C07 (IFRS~9 at \rsCSevenRatio{} of the Chilean provision) all come from conventions of treatment and not from a better learner. The same holds for the default definition: the long-run probability of default of the weakest grade is \pxOneRegCerepLong{}\,\% under one page of the same agency and \pxOneRegTransLong{}\,\% under another. A validation that reports one number without the convention behind it reports a quantity that a reader cannot reproduce.

\paragraph{Stress windows and planted defects defeat the usual tests.} In the equity crisis of 2007--09 no model of the value-at-risk ladder is eligible (\mkEligGfc{} of \mkNModels{}), against \mkEligCalm{} in the calm window, and the regulatory attribution test misses the model with a stale volatility in \rsCThirteenStale{}\,\% of its replications while a complement built on the scale and the level of the profit and loss catches it in \pxThirteenPowerRate{}\,\%. These are not failures of the engine. They are the property of the tests of the regulation, whose power against the defect that matters is not stated by the instrument that prescribes them, and that is the measurement of C22 repeated in each family.

\paragraph{A light is a triage, not a conclusion.} Of the \rsLightsTotal{} tests of the reports, \rsLightsRedShare{}\,\% end red, and the share runs from \rsRedLowShare{}\,\% in \rsRedLowCase{} to \rsRedHighShare{}\,\% in \rsRedHighCase{}. Part of it is a real defect and part is the instrument: the Hosmer--Lemeshow test with $G-2$ degrees of freedom on a validation sample rejects \rsCTwentyTwoHl{} of null samples, and the stability index above 0.10 fires in \rsCTwentyTwoPsi{} of unchanged samples of 100. The product therefore recommends reading a light together with the measured size of the test and its power against the defect in question, and reading the opinion level of a case as a statement about open findings. A level 3 follows from any significant finding, and \rsFindOpenShare{}\,\% of the findings are open; the register of the product has no bank that owns a model and remediates a finding, so the status of a finding records the rule that applies to it and not an action taken.

\paragraph{Between-method differences can be smaller than within-method dispersion.} In the operational-risk case the loss-distribution capital of one history is \rsCSeventeenLda{} million euros against \rsCSeventeenSma{} million for the standardised measurement approach, but the interquartile range of the loss-distribution capital is \pxTwelveIqrMin{} to \pxTwelveIqrMax{} times the truth over independent histories. A comparison of two methods on the one history a bank has is not informative, and the product reports the dispersion beside the median.

\paragraph{Negative and partial results of the proposed methods.} Two of the four proposed methods meet the criterion fixed before their evaluation: the complement to the attribution test (power \pxThirteenPowerRate{}\,\% at a size of \pxThirteenSize{}\,\%) and the conformal recalibration of the value at risk (\pxSixteenCellsHold{} of \pxSixteenCells{} cells). The anchored tipping-point probability meets its coverage clause but fails its stability clause on the agency's cohorts, which says that the multiplier it estimates absorbs the difference between two definitions of default, and the sequential monitors hold their size and detect earlier but lose power against one reading of the Kupiec test at day 250. These are results. A product that reported only the methods that worked would repeat the practice that its first section criticises.

\paragraph{When the product recommends what.} Use the scorecard as the benchmark and a learned challenger when a paired test supports the gain and the calibration is checked out of time; report every aggregate capital figure with the band of dependence assumptions; state the conversion factor and the default definition beside every parameter; read a back-test in a stress window only with the number of days and the expected exceedances; and treat the findings count as a measure of how much was tested, not of how bad the model is.
