\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{What the product does not do.} Contraste certifies, approves or declares no model compliant with any supervisory rule: a passed test is evidence, not compliance, and the opinion level of a case is the output of a stated rule on the open findings. It represents no real bank. The synthetic institution Banco Andino and every synthetic balance sheet, deposit population and loss history are labelled so, and the cases that start from real aggregates (the balance sheet of the bank that failed in 2023, the call reports, the Chilean aggregates) use them as public aggregates with synthetic behaviour. It scores nobody and supports no decision about a person. It is not a real-time system and it has no inventory of the models of an institution. Case \rsCaseNoResult{} is planned and has no result.

\paragraph{Assumptions that bound the results.} The results hold under the assumptions of each method, stated in its section: the one-factor Vasicek structure for correlation, the Markov structure for transition matrices, a severity family that the tail estimators assume (which favours them in the operational-risk cases), the exchangeability of the calibration and test windows for conformal methods, and the generators and severity ladders of the validation of the validator, which are design choices of the product. A rate measured on a generator is not the probability of detecting a real bank's problem. The 5 and 1 percent levels, the thresholds of the stability index, the deadlines and the severity scale of the findings register, the opinion rule and the internal limits against which some lights are coloured are conventions, not regulatory limits, and the report shows them as such.

\paragraph{The data the product lacks.} The card-default sample (C01) is a single snapshot with no out-of-time validation, and the corporate sample (C02) makes no out-of-time claim; the firm overlap between its yearly files is unknown, so a transfer across files is labelled exploratory. The mortgage cases rest on the small-business twin because the terms of the mortgage data set for derived aggregates in a public site are unconfirmed. The data of several sources are one country or one state (the fair-lending case reads the decisions of one state, and the public data carry no loan outcome), so the disparity measured is a disparity in decisions and not evidence of discrimination, and no legal claim is made. The rating agencies' own studies are linked and never mirrored, so the rating cases read the public cohorts of the European register, whose definition of a default is not common across agencies. The case of the supervisory stress replication has no data committed.

\paragraph{Gaps in the evidence of the research.} The research found no primary source for a supervisory fine or capital add-on tied by name to weak model validation, nor for the size of the model inventories or the validation backlogs of banks, so the executive argument rests on supervisory findings and dates and not on those figures. The mapping of the model-risk owner to a job title is a judgement. The publication date of the artificial intelligence act omnibus, the entry into force of one delegated act and the United Kingdom output-floor schedule rest on search summaries, and the Chilean phase-in percentage of the second pillar rests on press reports because the site of the central bank blocks scripted access. The claims that no maintained open toolkit provides an engine or a test catalogue are absence claims limited to the searches of the research on their stated dates.

\paragraph{Where the reconciliations stop.} The collateralised and initial-margin variants of the counterparty exposure do not reconcile with the reference engine (Section~\ref{sec:validation}), the wrong-way variant has no reconciliation because the example of the reference engine is a different model, and the equality with the transition-matrix cohort estimator is tested by the engine and not rerun by the notebooks of the product. The floors of the Chilean interest-rate standard are implemented and tested, but none binds on the committed curves, so their effect on a number is not demonstrated, and there is no second implementation to reconcile with.

\paragraph{Limits of the evidence on the proposed methods.} The anchored tipping-point probability is conditional on the equivalence of the two definitions of default of its two sources, and its stability criterion fails on the agency's cohorts. The sequential monitors lose power against one reading of the Kupiec test. The results of the proposed methods are measured on generators of known truth and on the committed windows; they are not claims about the models of any bank. The French equity series used by the conformal recalibration is derived-only, so only its outputs are committed, and the recalibration runs on the device's data root.

\paragraph{Reading the findings.} The register counts findings, and a count grows with the number of tests and variants a case runs, so it compares cases badly. The lights inherit the size of their tests, and \rsCTwentyTwoWithin{} of \pxOneTests{} tests hold it. The live lane runs ports whose parity with the engine is tested on the committed values; the report does not measure the web.
