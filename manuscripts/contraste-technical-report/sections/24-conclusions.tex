\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

Contraste answers, for \rsCasesResult{} cases with committed results, what a bank's risk model says, whether it passes the tests that a validator and a supervisor would run, what it changes in capital, provisions, liquidity or decisions, and where its limits lie. The validation raised \rsFindTotal{} findings, \rsFindSOne{} of them critical and \rsFindSTwo{} significant, and every case ends at an opinion level of 3 or 4 on the four-level scale, so no model of the study is free of a significant finding; the opinion is the output of a stated rule and is not an approval.

The numbers behind the answer are of three kinds. Ranking is not calibration: the scorecard of C03 keeps an AUC of \rsCThreeAuc{} while the observed five-year probability of default is \rsCThreeObs{}\,\% against \rsCThreePred{}\,\% predicted. The treatment of the estimate moves the result as much as the model: the risk weight of one book runs from \rsCFiveRwMin{}\,\% to \rsCFiveRwMax{}\,\% of the exposure with the calibration approach. And the tests themselves have error rates that the regulation does not state: \rsCTwentyTwoWithin{} of \pxOneTests{} tests hold their size, and the lights of the \rsReports{} reports are \rsLightsRedShare{}\,\% red, part of it the property of the instrument.

Of the four methods the product proposes, the complement to the attribution test meets its criterion (\pxThirteenPowerRate{}\,\% power at a size of \pxThirteenSize{}\,\%) but its size does not transfer to desks with another volatility process; the conformal recalibration of the value at risk moves the hit rates towards the target and meets its criterion in all \pxSixteenCells{} cells on the asymptotic p-values, but in \pxPpSixteenHoneGain{} of them against the null of the wrapped count; the anchored tipping-point probability fails two of its clauses, the coverage of the existing treatments and its stability on the agency's cohorts; and the sequential monitors lose power at day 250, fail their delay clause at three times the nominal hit rate and, for the default probabilities, lose their size when defaults are correlated. The next steps are the replication of the supervisory stress model (case \rsCaseNoResult{}), a second implementation of the interest-rate floors, against which they could be reconciled, and the measurement of the deploy payload and of the browser check on the full set of built cases, which the release run has yet to record.
