\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Internal validity}

Agreement between two routes shows that they compute the same thing, not that the thing is the right one. Three of the checks hold less than they appear to. The IRB capital of the credit-card book is equal to the SciPy formula to the last digit because the engine and the formula call the same normal distribution function, so the check holds the wiring of the formula and nothing about the distribution function. The QuantLib present value is the same exponential discounting as the engine's and agrees to \irrQlMax{}; it holds the time convention and the bucket wiring and not the choice of continuous compounding, and the defect of the ablation that replaces it by annual compounding is found for that reason and not because QuantLib is an independent model of the cash flows. The Jeffreys transcription is accurate to \ecbJeffMax{}, the accuracy of a quadrature, so a defect smaller than that in the engine's Beta function call would pass. A second limit is that the transcription of the ECB statistics was written for this study from the specification as extracted, and a misreading of the specification that was shared by the engine and the transcription would pass both; the specification's loss and exposure formulas were not transcribed because the engine does not implement them. The ablation's defects were chosen by the experimenter and are \ablMutants{} in number, so the detection counts describe those defects and are not an estimate of the detection rate over the defects that a developer makes. The hypotheses were written by the author and fixed in the same revision as the results (Section~\ref{sec:experimental-design}), so no result was predicted in advance in a way a reader can verify. Hypothesis~H8 measures the coverage of the check sets and not the sensitivity of different kinds of reference to the same defect: each unique detection follows from which engine function a layer exercises (the Jeffreys and concentration statistics only in layer $B$, the present value only in layer $E$, the correlation estimator only in layer $D$, the large-portfolio functions only in layer $A$, and any change of the sample path in layer $C$), and only two defects are seen by two layers. Layer B applies the transcription to the Jeffreys and the concentration statistics only, and layer E runs at reduced size, so their detections understate what the full experiments of Section~\ref{sec:results} would find.

\subsection{External validity and scope}

The real-outcome data are the credit-card holdout, used for the Jeffreys comparison, and the charge-off rates that fit the loan book; the loan book itself is constructed and the interest rate risk bank is synthetic, so no result concerns the model of an actual bank. The portfolio checks cover one book of \(1{,}120\) names and one correlation structure in one sector for the CreditRisk+ recursion; multi-sector recursions, random loss given default, the Student $t$ copula's importance sampler and migration-mode models are not implemented or not checked. The correlation grids are read as committed and only three cells were re-run, with \cellReps{} replications each, so the cell that falls outside the band is known to fall outside from the committed 400 replications and is confirmed in direction, not in size, by the re-run. The shock-size tables of the IRRBB engine are not compared in this study with the primary tables of the standard; only the worked example is. The ECB statistics for the loss given default and the exposure parameters are absent from the engine, and the statement of Section~\ref{sec:results} that the package implements the probability-of-default statistics is a statement about the registry at one release. The list of the \ecbSections{} statistics is the author's reading of Sections 2.5 to 2.10 of the instructions: one entry for each statistic, one for each group of qualitative validation tools (2.5.2, 2.6.4, 2.9.2 and 2.9.5) and one for the slotting section, of which only the headings were read and not the content, so whether it fixes a statistic that could be implemented is not confirmed. The count of absent statistics depends on that grouping, and the mapping of each entry to the registry is made by hand and only confirmed, for the implemented ones, by the registry's identifiers.

\subsection{Construct validity and the absence claim}

The motivating claim that no open toolkit implements these tests as specified is a claim of absence, and absence is established only for the places searched. The survey read the metadata (summary and long description) of \pypiFound{} packages from a list written by the authors, on \pypiDate{}; a package that implements a test without naming it in its description would not be found, a package outside the list would not be found, and GitHub was not searched by this study. The scan covers the Python sources of the installed distributions of one environment; compiled modules, such as the QuantLib core, are not scanned. The earlier search that the claim rests on, of GitHub and PyPI on the 4th of October 2026 and of CRAN and GitHub for portfolio models, is not repeated here, and the claim is stated as holding for maintained, permissively licensed implementations on those dates. The $z$ comparisons use the normal approximation with \mcReps{} runs, for which a Student distribution would give wider limits; the largest $|z|$ reported is below one, so the conclusion does not depend on it, but the minimum detectable shifts of Proposition~\ref{prop:power} are approximate. Finally, the 99\,\% band used for the coverage cells treats the 24 cells as independent comparisons with no correction for multiplicity; with a Bonferroni correction over 24 cells at a family-wise level of 5\,\% the cutoff of $|z|$ is 3.08 and the cell at $z=\gridMlWorstZ{}$ still lies outside it, but at a family-wise level of 1\,\% the cutoff is 3.53 and it lies inside. The refutation of Hypothesis~H5 for a single cell therefore holds at the level at which the hypothesis was stated and is not robust to a stricter family-wise correction. The evidence against the hypothesis does not rest on that cell alone: the coverage is below nominal in \gridMlBelow{} of \gridMlCells{} cells and the pooled coverage is $\gridMlPooledZ{}$ standard errors from $0.95$, but the pooled figure treats the cells as independent, and the cells share a simulator and, for the 22-year grid, one seed, so it is an indication and not a test.
