\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

The code, the generator, the committed C13 documents and the scripts of the study are in the repository \path{github.com/fsantibanezleal/CAOS_Contraste}, in the folder \path{manuscripts/scale-and-level-complement-to-the-pla-test}, at
release v1.00.000 of CAOS\_Contraste (tag \texttt{v1.00.000}, \url{https://github.com/fsantibanezleal/CAOS_Contraste/releases/tag/v1.00.000}), which holds the three result files of this paper (\path{results.json}, \path{results_theory.json} and \path{c22_size_check.json}) and the generator with which they were produced; the engine release is named below.\ % voice-ok
The statistics come from the open-source engine \texttt{riskvalidation} 1.1.0, published on the Python Package Index, in the modules \path{market.frtb}, \path{market.pla_complement} and \path{validation.market}; the generator is \path{data-pipeline/pipeline/cases/c13/generator.py} (\path{desk-pnl-generator/1.0.0}), and the committed documents with which the statistics were reconciled are \path{data/derived/C13}.
No external dataset is used.
The experiments run from the root of the repository with the environment of the product, in this order, and each command rebuilds what follows it; \path{EXP} stands for the folder \path{manuscripts/scale-and-level-complement-to-the-pla-test}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \texttt{python}~\path{EXP/experiments/simulate.py} simulates 63 blocks of 1,000 desk-years and caches the statistic rows; with 24 worker processes on a 32-core workstation it took 79 seconds.
\item \texttt{python}~\path{EXP/experiments/analyse.py} reads the cache, computes every rate, interval, area, ablation row and check, and writes \path{results.json} in 85 seconds; its last line states whether the engine's counts equal the committed documents.
\item \texttt{python}~\path{EXP/experiments/theory_check.py} simulates the noise-free model on windows of 250 and of 100,000 days and writes \path{results_theory.json} in 30 seconds.
\item \texttt{python}~\path{EXP/experiments/c22_size_check.py} reads the size of the Christoffersen test in the committed C22 document, reruns the engine function on the same design and at the design of the desk back-test, and writes \path{c22_size_check.json}; the run time of the script, imports included, is recorded in that file and was \ccheckSeconds{} seconds.
\item \texttt{python}~\path{EXP/make_tables.py} and \texttt{python}~\path{EXP/make_figures.py} write the tables and the figures from the three result files; no number in them is typed.
\item \texttt{python -m pytest}~\path{EXP/experiments} runs the 19 tests of the comparators, of the decision rule against the engine's own calibration, of the closed forms against long simulations and of the C22 size check.
\item \texttt{pdflatex}, \texttt{bibtex} and two further \texttt{pdflatex} passes build the document.
\end{enumerate}
Every random draw comes from the seeds of Section~\ref{sec:design-data} (case seeds 13, 23, 33, 43, 53, 63 and 5013 to 5022, with the seed rule $1000s+1000k+i$), from the seed 0 of the bootstrap of the area under the curve, from the seed 20261107 of the C22 size check, from the seed 20261104 of the Gaussian check of Proposition~\ref{prop:size} and from the seeds 20261104 plus the replication index of the simulation of the closed forms, so that the outputs are deterministic.
The three result files hold about \resultsKB, \theoryKB{} and \ccheckKB{} kilobytes.
The cache of statistic rows is not committed; it is regenerated by the first command.
